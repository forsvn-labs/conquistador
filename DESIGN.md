# Design

The public product is a CLI, skill, MCP server and app API. Its main design surface is the sequence of prompts, previews and handoffs. Use a short first command with a visible next step. Distinguish a draft, an approval and verified delivery, and preserve keyboard and plain-text paths. The [product contract](docs/PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md) owns behavior and [BRAIN](BRAIN.md) routes authorized collaborators to design originals. No shared visual tokens are defined here; the marketing site owns its own design.
