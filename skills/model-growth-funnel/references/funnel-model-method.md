# Build an inspectable growth decision model

Use this method when the user needs to decide whether a growth target is feasible or which constraint to address. Read available initiatives and evidence first. If the task lacks a baseline, build an explicitly assumed scenario rather than pretending that a benchmark is the user's result.

## Define and calculate

Use [event-defined stages](funnel-models.md) to select compatible populations, events, and periods. Work backward from the requested outcome through the necessary transitions. Show formulas, units, and rounded operational volumes. List each assumption with its source or owner and the evidence needed to replace it.

Map each initiative to an event or delay and explain how it could affect the result. Keep the user's desired target separate from the model's forecast or scenario output. A proposed target is not evidence of feasibility.

## Compare choices

Compare the current approach with feasible alternatives, such as changing the target period, improving a specific transition, reducing the scope, or deferring the initiative. Evaluate the same outcome and constraints for each. Recommend the choice supported by the evidence and show what would change that recommendation.

Use [unit economics](unit-economics.md) for explicit costs and [benchmark review](benchmarks.md) for any external comparison. Unknown costs remain unknown. Required returns or spending limits come from the user's context and authority, not an installed universal threshold.

## Stress the recommendation

Use [stress tests](stress-tests.md) for downside, base, and upside cases. Identify the assumptions that change the decision and test capacity at the required volumes. Show the consequences of delay, incomplete retention observation, or a failed dependency when relevant.

## Deliver

Follow [format conventions](format-conventions.md). Include a feasible range, revised target, or explicit no-go recommendation with its reason. Name the smallest evidence-gathering step that resolves a material uncertainty. Keep the next step within available authority.

The model supports a decision. It does not report an unrun campaign, guarantee a forecast, or authorize spending. Use [sequential fallback](../fallbacks/sequential.md) when the host lacks independent agents.
