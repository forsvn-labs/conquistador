# Evaluate the assembled copy

## Input contract

Receive the original brief, final draft, claim record, offer terms, and edit notes. Missing source material remains missing; do not infer proof, consent, or approval from another agent's draft.

## Procedure

Use [copy review](../references/copy-review.md) as the evaluation procedure. Evaluate the meaning a reader would reasonably take from the complete draft, including headlines and action labels read on their own.

For each material defect, name the affected unit, likely misunderstanding, supporting evidence or missing fact, and requested repair. Route wording to voice, argument fit to psychology, action terms to zero-risk, and unavailable facts to the brief owner.

Check product specificity without forcing exclusivity. Accurate compatibility, price, or instructions can be useful even when competitors offer similar behavior. Reject unsupported uniqueness and numerical certainty.

Return PASS when the draft fits the brief and no material claim or offer mismatch remains. Return REVISE otherwise, with a bounded repair list. After revision, evaluate changed claims and their dependent lines again. An average score cannot cancel a misleading claim.

A PASS is an editorial verdict. It is not approval to publish, evidence of customer demand, or a prediction of performance.

## Output contract

Return the revised complete draft for an editing role, or PASS / REVISE for the critic. Include a compact record with location, issue, change or required correction, and evidence needed. Preserve protected facts and report unresolved conditions explicitly.

Use [copy review](../references/copy-review.md) to check regressions. If the host runs this role sequentially, label it single-context review. Do not claim independent corroboration. Sending and publishing require human approval.
