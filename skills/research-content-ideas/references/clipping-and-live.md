# Long-form source and clip distribution plan

Use this method when the user has rights to a longer recording, or a justified reason to produce one, and needs to decide whether excerpts can support a campaign. Audience fit must come from the user's evidence. Age, gender or a media brand is not sufficient evidence that a clip network will reach the intended buyer.

## Source decision

Inventory available recordings with owner, duration, topic, permitted edits, participant consent and allowed distribution. Choose a specific task, question or demonstration relevant to the campaign. A source can be an explanation, interview, product session or event; it does not need a named entertainment or debate format.

For each candidate excerpt, record start/end, the claim, the context needed to understand it and any conditions that an edit could conceal. Exclude excerpts that change the speaker's meaning, expose private information or require rights that are not available.

## Compare distribution routes

| Route | Decision to resolve | Required handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owned accounts | Can the existing audience use this excerpt, and can the team edit and respond? | Source timecodes, adapted assets, captions, destination and response owner. |
| Approved creator accounts | Does a named creator reach the intended audience with a suitable relationship? | Written scope, disclosures, edit/paid-use rights, review owner and report fields. |
| Paid clip distribution | Can the operator inspect accounts, cap cost and reject invalid or unsuitable placements? | Eligible accounts/regions, allowed clips, payment definition, fraud review, per-clip caps and termination terms. |
| Live session | Does the task benefit from live demonstration or questions? | Run of show, participant permissions, moderation, recovery plan and recording rights. |

Live selling and educational or awareness sessions need different outcomes. For selling, define the order path, availability and fulfillment terms. For an educational session, define the task or useful next action. Do not measure either only by total viewers.

## Test the source before wider distribution

1. Select an excerpt whose meaning survives outside the full recording.
2. Produce a draft with enough setup, accessible captions and the intended next action.
3. Have a reviewer unfamiliar with the full recording describe the claim and conditions. Revise if important context is missing.
4. Estimate actual editing, review and response cost. Compare this with the campaign's resource cap and expected useful outcome.
5. Prepare a bounded distribution test with approved accounts, observation period and stop conditions. Do not assume an organic result predicts paid placement.
6. After an authorized test, inspect clip-level placement, audience relevance, rights compliance and qualified outcomes before expanding.

No fixed clip-per-hour target, minimum learning spend, CPM range or demographic conversion claim is supplied. A source with few usable excerpts may still justify a small campaign; a source with many clips may still reach the wrong audience.

## Cost and measurement

Calculate estimated cost from the actual quote, eligible view definition, editing/review fees and explicit caps. Distinguish contracted billable views from independently verified audience exposure. Require clip URL, account, publication time, region data where available, paid support and reporting period. Do not add view totals across accounts and call them unique people.

Record the business outcome, diagnostic engagement measure, baseline and attribution limitations. A referral parameter can identify a click; it cannot measure everyone who saw a clip. Stop on unauthorized placement, missing disclosure, disputed rights, uninspectable reporting or the approved resource limit.

## Campaign output

The Channel Selection row names the route, specific audience evidence, assigned message, permitted source, resource cap and next test. The execution brief includes clip-level manifests, approval owner, response coverage, measurement definitions and the condition for keeping, revising or stopping the route. Publication and any paid commitment require separate exact authorization.
