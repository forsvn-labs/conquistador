# Cross-platform research comparison

Compare the decisions a producer must make for the requested audience and account. The platform methods supply inspection questions, not universal performance facts. Keep unavailable destinations out of the recommendation and explain why.

## Comparison worksheet

Use only the platforms requested. Each cell identifies its evidence type and uncertainty.

| Decision | TikTok | Instagram Reels | YouTube Shorts | X video, opt-in | LinkedIn video, opt-in |

These are candidate inspection tasks. They do not require a specific genre, duration, audio treatment, caption count or engagement tactic.

## Evidence in cells

Distinguish these categories explicitly:

- `technical constraint`: cite the exact checked requirement, its source, account or route scope and inspection date. Do not generalize an ad specification to an organic upload.
- `sample observation`: cite inspected items, sample size, capture window and access limits. A visible metric is descriptive; differing account audiences prevent a causal comparison.
- `proposed test`: name the uncertain choice, comparison and outcome needed to resolve it. The recommendation is untested until relevant results exist.
- `unknown`: say what was not observed and how that limits the decision.

Do not fill a missing cell with a memorized ranking rule. Preserve `mechanics_sources_verified[]` for checked technical evidence only. Neither this worksheet nor the per-platform method is an external source verification.

## Choosing a lead destination

Start with the viewer task, account access, available assets and valid action route. Eliminate destinations that cannot support the requested outcome or permitted production. For the remaining options, identify the decision that evidence can resolve at an acceptable cost.

Recommend a lead destination only when the recorded fit supports it. If evidence is insufficient, give a bounded comparison test and state what would change the choice. Regional labels, industry labels and platform popularity alone do not determine the choice.

## Handoff checks

Each recommendation must identify its platform-specific production or route decision and cite its supporting observation or technical constraint. Include the proposed test and success measure where the choice remains uncertain. Shared accessibility checks may appear across platforms; specificity comes from the actual account and artifact, not from inventing a unique platform rule.
