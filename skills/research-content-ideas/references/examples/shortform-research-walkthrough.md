# Short-form research walkthrough

This is a hypothetical process example. No searches, video inspections, platform checks or account tests occurred. The placeholders below are instructions for a future authorized run, not source records or findings. No real account, audience quote, metric or track is represented.

## Setup and scope

The operator wants ideas for a video explaining how to diagnose a failed software setup. The requested destinations are TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. The audience hint is people attempting that setup; their preferred tone and regional behavior are unknown.

The research task is to identify where existing explanations lose necessary context and propose a testable production choice. X and LinkedIn remain outside this run. The scout uses supplied competitor seeds if available and records search terms, selection reasons and inaccessible items.

## Scout evidence

For each inspected item, the scout records the actual URL, capture date, account, publication date when available and observable metrics. It describes the opening only if footage was inspected. A thumbnail can identify an object but cannot establish the spoken explanation, audio or cut sequence.

The capture asks whether the viewer can identify the failed state, follow the corrective action and verify the result. A source pair may differ in one of these respects. The scout records other differences that prevent a causal performance comparison.

Counts and flags follow the existing workflow contract. They describe how much was captured, not statistical confidence. Missing retention or conversion data remains unobserved. A visible reaction count does not reveal platform ranking logic.

## Extraction and synthesis

The extractor groups inspectable similarities while retaining source URLs, context, rights limits and counterexamples. An observation about setup visibility can support a production question. It does not justify copying a creator's opening, caption or explanation.

The synthesis checks the platform methods for the decisions needed in the brief:

| Destination | Inspection question | Proposed comparison if evidence supports it |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | Can a viewer identify the failed setup before the corrective action? | Compare a visible failed state with the current verbal setup while holding the answer stable |
| Instagram Reels | Does the intended crop preserve the error and the evidence of correction? | Compare crops of the same demonstration and inspect legibility before interpreting engagement |
| YouTube Shorts | Does the short resolve a question independently, and what does the linked lesson add? | Compare a complete standalone answer with one that also offers the relevant next lesson |

These are candidate tests. The run selects only those supported by the actual brief and inspected evidence. It does not need to invent different timing, music or ranking rules for each platform.

## Critic review and correction

Suppose the draft tells a producer that a completion target will unlock reach. The critic fails that unsupported claim and routes it to synthesis. The correction removes the claimed platform effect and specifies the actual uncertainty, available outcome measure and comparison. Adding a platform homepage citation would not resolve the failure.

If a crop recommendation points to obscured evidence and a placement preview, it is actionable even when another platform also needs a crop check. The critic checks the task and evidence rather than counting unique platform tactics.

A clean structural review does not supply missing source or execution evidence. Unresolved material gaps retain the contract's concerns or blocked status. No pass verdict is claimed for this hypothetical run.

## Production and evaluation handoff

`create-shortform` receives the selected decision, source context, sample flags, rights limits and test contract. It writes original production language. It previews the artifact and confirms the destination before publication is authorized.

`evaluate-shortform` compares the resulting artifact with the viewer task and agreed outcome measure. It preserves missing metrics and confounding conditions. It does not grade the result against an inherited reach multiplier or treat the research catalog as proof that a tactic should perform.
