# Cancellation and payment message drafts

Adapt these original drafts only to verified account facts and approved terms. They are unsent drafts. A retention plan does not authorize customer contact, retries or account changes. Use the [churn playbook](churn-playbook.md) to define the event conditions, owner and stop rules.

## Drafting checks

Confirm the actual event, subscription state, effective date, accessible destination and contact permission. Remove unsupported claims about product benefit or customer intent. Do not state that data is safe, access continues or a retry will occur unless those facts are established.

Make the requested cancellation clear and available. A reason survey or offer is optional. Do not insert an arbitrary sequence of messages or delay cancellation while collecting feedback.

## Cancellation confirmation

```text
Subject: Your cancellation confirmation

Hi [name],

Your [subscription or service] cancellation [verified status].
It takes effect on [verified date].

[Accurate access, billing and export information relevant to this account.]

You can review the details at [verified destination].
If you need help with this request, use [support route].

[Sender]
```

## Optional reason request

```text
Subject: Optional feedback on your cancellation

Hi [name],

If you would like to share what led to your cancellation, you can reply
or use [optional feedback route]. Your response will help us understand
which part of [customer task] needs attention.

This request does not change your cancellation status.

[Sender]
```

Use this only when the account state supports that final sentence. Preserve the customer's words and permissions rather than converting a reply into a reusable testimonial.

## Relevant alternative

```text
Subject: An option for [stated need]

Hi [name],

You mentioned [verified need]. We can offer [available alternative]
with these terms: [price, duration, limitations and what happens afterward].

If that fits your needs, [clear acceptance route]. Otherwise,
[accurate statement of how the cancellation proceeds].

[Sender]
```

Do not fill the need from a guessed risk score. Offer support or a workaround only when it is available. Do not promise a roadmap date or claim the alternative will resolve an untested problem.

## Payment action required

```text
Subject: Payment action needed for [subscription]

Hi [name],

[Verified payment event] for [invoice or service reference].
[Accurate amount and current status, where appropriate.]

You can review or update the payment details at [verified secure route].
[Actual next step or effective date, if one is established.]

If the record looks wrong, contact [support route].

[Sender]
```

A payment status must be checked before sending. Suppress this draft if the issue has resolved, the invoice is invalid or the event is unknown. Do not imply an attempt occurred when only a retry proposal exists.

## Account change notice

```text
Subject: [Verified account change] on [effective date]

Hi [name],

Your [service] will [actual approved change] on [date] because
[accurate account condition].

[What access, billing or stored data changes, using verified terms.]
[Available resolution route, if applicable.]

[Sender]
```

Use the actual terms and timing. A deadline is not a persuasion device. The message must not imply data deletion, suspension or reactivation rights that are not part of the account's real state and approved process.

## Review and measurement

Record which event made the draft eligible and which event should suppress it. Preview every destination and placeholder. Evaluate useful resolutions, support burden, complaints and completed cancellations as appropriate to the task. Opens, clicks and an accepted offer alone do not prove successful retention.

Compare message alternatives only within an authorized test with defined cohorts, outcomes and review conditions. No subject line, contact count or timing sequence here carries a claimed response advantage.
