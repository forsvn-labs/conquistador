# Editorial polish method

Use privately after `write-copy` or another creation outcome as the final voice and clarity pass for
marketing work.

Preserve the author's specific experience, vocabulary, rhythm, claims, protected terms, and intent
while removing generic framing, repetition, inflated abstraction, manufactured vulnerability, and
unnecessary length. Judge language in context; this is editorial judgment, not a deterministic phrase
deny-list or detector-evasion exercise.

Load the recovered catalog, not a detector:

- [AI writing patterns](../references/ai-patterns.md)
- [human-writing stylebook](../references/human-writing-stylebook.md)
- [voice injection](../references/voice-injection.md)
- [conciseness rules](../references/conciseness-rules.md)
- recovered specialists under [editorial-polish](../references/editorial-polish/) (pattern scanner,
  strip, compression, soul injection, voice extractor, critic)

Cut those tells without stripping proof, protected tokens, mechanism, or CTA force. Do not run
detector-evasion loops. Historical `detector-resistance` and Pangram gates stay in GitHub history.

After editing, recheck factual claims, quoted language, names, numbers, links, intentional stylistic
choices, and channel fit. Apply Vietnamese polish through the dedicated language outcome and standard
when relevant. Return the revised work first and explain only consequential changes.
