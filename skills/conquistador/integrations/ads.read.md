# ads.read

Read ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, creatives, spend, and results.

## Answers

- Which campaigns and creatives drive results at what cost?
- Where is spend wasted, and where does a budget move help?
- How did a test compare to the control?

## Find the tool

Search phrases: `ads campaign insights spend`, `list ad campaigns`, `ad performance report`.

Name the account, the date range, and the level (campaign, ad set, ad). Keep the currency and
the attribution window in the result.

## Providers

| Provider | Search for | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta Ads | `insights` on the ad account (`act_<id>`) | Ad account ID, level, fields, time range |
| Google Ads | `googleAds search` (GAQL) | Customer ID, GAQL query, login customer ID for managers |
| LinkedIn Ads | `adAnalytics` | Ad account URN, pivot, date range |
| TikTok Ads | `report integrated get` | Advertiser ID, dimensions, metrics |
| Reddit, X, Microsoft Ads | `reports` or `campaign stats` | Account ID |
