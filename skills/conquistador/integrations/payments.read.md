# payments.read

Read revenue, subscriptions, customers, plans, refunds, and churn.

## Answers

- What are MRR, new revenue, expansion, and churn over a period?
- Which plans and prices convert, and which churn?
- How did a price change affect trials and upgrades?

## Find the tool

Search phrases: `list subscriptions`, `revenue charges report`, `list customers payments`.

Report totals and rates. Do not copy customer names or emails into deliverables.
