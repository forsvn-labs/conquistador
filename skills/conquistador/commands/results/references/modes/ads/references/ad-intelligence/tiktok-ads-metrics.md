# Evaluate TikTok Ads delivery and outcomes

This is a campaign decision procedure. It supplies no performance benchmark or
claim of current platform verification. Record the source, date, placement, region,
and account scope of platform constraints before using them. If verification is
unavailable, deliver a draft with the affected setup decisions unresolved.

Keep Spark and in-feed identity, placement, audience, video version, offer, and
attribution window in each comparison. Preserve exact view-event labels from the
export; do not silently rename a metric to a standard opening-view duration.

| Signal | Use and limit |
|---|---|
| Video starts and timed views | Report duration, count, and denominator for each rate |
| Completion and watch duration | Locate abandonment without inferring purchase intent |
| Clicks, conversions, CPA | Trace delivery to the declared event with lag and attribution limits |
| Reach and frequency | Describe exposure; do not assign a universal fatigue threshold |
| Revenue or GMV and ROAS | Use only for an in-scope, measured commerce outcome |

When opening views fall, inspect placement mix and video versions before selecting
a new opening. When later retention falls, inspect the corresponding sequence.
Good viewing metrics with poor conversions can reflect destination friction, offer
fit, tracking, or unqualified attention. Preserve these competing explanations.

Recommend a refresh only as a test of a named component. Define controls, loss
ceiling, and evidence needed to reverse the recommendation. Neither a few days of
results nor a strong hold rate supplies a universal keep/kill rule.

## Decision contract

Read ../measurement-over-heuristics.md before assigning confidence. Missing evidence
is missing, never zero. Keep observed results, attribution, and causal inference
separate. Recommendations cannot authorize live account changes.
