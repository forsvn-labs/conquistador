# Worked evaluation decisions

These invented arithmetic examples are specifications, not live calls, observed
customer results, or release evidence. Their amounts and rules are not defaults.

## Comparable outcome, uncertain cause

A fictional Meta cold-audience packet reports 240 currency units of spend and 12
qualified outcomes after its declared lag. The matched baseline reports 200 units
and 10 qualified outcomes. Both cost 20 units per qualified outcome. The test
contract accepts cost at or below 25, a total loss ceiling of 300, and requires
completion of the declared follow-up period. Those are example operator choices.

Ingest confirms the dates, eligibility, event definition, and denominators. Raw
CTR declined while reported frequency rose, but placement mix also changed.
Diagnosis records fatigue and placement mix as competing explanations. Stable
qualified-outcome cost does not establish creative lift or exclude fatigue.

Recommendation is keep within existing authority if the declared information
requirement is met, with review of the placement split. It does not prescribe a
hook refresh from CTR alone. If uncertainty could reverse the cost verdict, the
recommendation remains provisional. Critic checks that the exact evidence rule,
not the number of conversions, supports the confidence label.

## Missing evidence and operational pause

A second packet has spend but no reliable outcome export because event mapping
changed. Ingest leaves outcomes missing. It does not calculate zero CPA or conclude
that every click failed. Recommendation is pause pending tracking reconciliation,
encoded blocked in the ledger with that precise action in the description.

## Zero observed outcomes

A complete packet at the agreed terminal loss ceiling reports zero outcomes after
the stated lag. CPA is undefined. The agreed loss rule supports stop and a discard
ledger row, while the performance estimate may remain uncertain. The evaluator
must not claim statistical inferiority just because the money limit was reached.

## Artifact and ledger review

For each cycle, write the Verdict, Evidence, What Changed This Cycle, Diagnosis,
Next Cycle Recommendation, Results Row, and Learning Promotion sections. Use
../format-conventions.md and its eight-column schema. Validate counts, scopes,
precise action, and row identity before append. Without a loop, return inline.

No example promotes a general fatigue threshold or platform benchmark. A later
controlled test may support a narrowly scoped learning with uncertainty and an
expiry condition. Critic review never authorizes the recommended live action.
