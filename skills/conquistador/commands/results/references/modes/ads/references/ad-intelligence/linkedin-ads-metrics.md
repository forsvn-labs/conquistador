# Evaluate LinkedIn Ads outcome quality

This is a campaign decision procedure. It supplies no performance benchmark or
claim of current platform verification. Record the source, date, placement, region,
and account scope of platform constraints before using them. If verification is
unavailable, deliver a draft with the affected setup decisions unresolved.

Compare by targeting_mode, offer, form or landing destination, and cohort date.
Match qualified leads and opportunities back to their acquisition cohorts where
permitted. Do not compare a mature cohort with one still awaiting sales review.

| Signal | Use and limit |
|---|---|
| Spend, clicks, CTR, CPC | Diagnose delivery using exact denominators; no assumed industry norm |
| Leads and form completions | Show form starts or other actual denominator; label unavailable fields |
| Qualified leads and opportunities | Apply the declared qualification criteria consistently |
| Cost per qualified outcome | Include the cohort's full acquisition spend and report unmatched outcomes |
| Reach and frequency | Inspect exposure alongside outcome changes and audience mix |

If form completions rise while qualified outcomes fall, check qualification changes,
CRM matching, review lag, targeting, and offer wording. A form design is one possible
cause, not a verdict. If frequency and costs rise, test audience mix and auction
changes before attributing the result to fatigue.

Use the account's outcome cost limit and time to qualification. No platform-wide
CTR, CPC, audience-size, or review-duration default applies. Recommend a bounded
form, message, or eligibility test only after naming evidence that could distinguish
it from competing explanations.

## Decision contract

Read ../measurement-over-heuristics.md before assigning confidence. Missing evidence
is missing, never zero. Keep observed results, attribution, and causal inference
separate. Recommendations cannot authorize live account changes.
