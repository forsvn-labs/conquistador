# Answer-content readiness checklist

Use this local review with `retrieval-layer-seo.md`. Readiness means a usable, supported answer
and explicit delivery status. It does not predict quotation, ranking or citation by any provider.

- Match the question and audience to an exact passage or identify the missing answer.
- Read the passage with only its necessary heading and qualifiers. Resolve ambiguous subjects,
  units, references, conditions and dates without forcing a word or sentence count.
- Check each factual claim against its supplied source and limitations. Do not add numbers,
  quotations, authorities or third-party mentions for appearance.
- For a comparison, use equivalent criteria, units and scope. For a procedure, name prerequisites,
  actions, completion checks and failure recovery. Use only the rows and sections the task needs.
- Check visible content against JSON-LD and any Markdown companion. Keep author, product,
  pricing and version facts consistent. Structured data has no assumed citation benefit.
- Record supplied crawler/index evidence separately from content quality. Identify the actual
  pathway under review; unknown access is pending, not proof of total invisibility.
- Preserve ownership, licenses and required attribution for source material.
- Specify the proposed change, acceptance check, owner and bounded future observation. Without
  captured provider evidence, citation behavior remains untested.

Report applicable checks as pass, fail or unknown with evidence. Skip inapplicable checks with a
reason. Do not sum them into a visibility score. Use the standard finding fields and retrieval
extension in format-conventions.md. Escalate relevant technical defects without withholding
useful offline content corrections. This checklist contains no platform preference model.
