# Bound claims to evidence

For each proposed claim, record the exact claim, source, date, scope, permission, and limitation.
Mark it supported, narrowed, or removed. Permission to inspect private evidence is not permission
to disclose a customer name, screenshot, amount, or quotation to a prospect.

| Evidence | Can support | Cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Working demonstration | Observed behavior under shown conditions | General reliability or customer results |
| Documented delivery | Work actually completed within its recorded scope | Identical future outcomes |
| Customer statement with disclosure permission | That customer's accurately attributed experience | Typical performance or universal satisfaction |
| Measured result with method and period | The bounded measurement | Causation or future guarantee |
| Operator statement of capability | A clearly labeled offered service or proposed scope | A proven result |
| Recipient observation | The observed event | Seller competence or recipient intent to buy |

Explicit no-pitch research needs no seller evidence because it makes no seller claim. Targeted
READY still requires a recipient signal. Without one, use a labeled NEEDS_SIGNAL generic template.
Commercial messages with weak selection evidence can be drafted conditionally but may not imply
an observed recipient problem. Unsupported claims are blocked even when other parts are usable.
