# no-pitch-research

State affiliation and genuine research intent. Ask one open question about current behavior. Do not include product superiority, promised outcomes, seller proof, an offer, demo, or a disguised qualification question. A real sourced recipient signal permits targeted READY. Missing signal yields a labeled generic NEEDS_SIGNAL template with no observed-person opener.

## Choose the next step

For one requested message, return only that message, selection/claim/action boundaries and pre-send gaps. No automatic follow-up, reply bank, or measurement package. Silence means no follow-up. Recipient control is explicit; a crafted template is still not a targeted sendable draft.

## Review boundary

Use [the method](../method.md) and [shared rubric](../copy-validation-rubric.md). Keep facts,
inferences, and proposed actions distinct. Do not fabricate relevance or evidence to satisfy a
score. All send, enrichment, CRM, and scheduling actions require separate exact action approval.
