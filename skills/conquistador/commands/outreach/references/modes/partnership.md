# partnership-sell

Explain the proposed contribution from each party, audience overlap supported by evidence, decision owner if known, and costs or dependencies. Do not assume shared customers, access to a distribution list, endorsement, or permission to use a logo.

## Craft

Open with the overlap fact (the shared audience or the gap one side fills for the other), then the
exact exchange: what each side gives and gets, in one or two lines. Make the first ask small, such
as whether a short brief is worth sending.

## Choose the next step

Ask whether the defined collaboration merits further discussion. Keep commercial terms, data sharing, and publication outside the drafting authority.

## Review boundary

Use [the method](../method.md) and [shared rubric](../copy-validation-rubric.md). Keep facts,
inferences, and proposed actions distinct. Do not fabricate relevance or evidence to satisfy a
score. All send, enrichment, CRM, and scheduling actions require separate exact action approval.
