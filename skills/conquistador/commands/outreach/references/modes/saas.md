# saas-sell

Describe the product behavior that exists now and any relevant access or integration prerequisite. Mark unavailable features and limitations. A demonstration can show behavior but does not establish savings, adoption, reliability, or customer results.

## Craft

Lead with the costly moment in the buyer's week or one peer result the sender may share, then the
product's one relevant behavior in a line. A sample of real output or a first run on the buyer's
own data is a strong second touch. Ask about current practice before asking for a demo.

## Choose the next step

A trial or demo request must state material commitments and known limits. Do not imply that replying creates an account or authorizes data access.

## Review boundary

Use [the method](../method.md) and [shared rubric](../copy-validation-rubric.md). Keep facts,
inferences, and proposed actions distinct. Do not fabricate relevance or evidence to satisfy a
score. All send, enrichment, CRM, and scheduling actions require separate exact action approval.
