# services-sell

Name the proposed work, who performs it, inputs needed, delivery boundary, and what is excluded. Distinguish a completed assessment from a proposed assessment. A past project supports only its documented scope; do not imply the recipient will receive the same business outcome.

When the offer is a diagnostic (audit, review, or assessment), lead with what the buyer gets, the delivery time, and the risk term. Present the diagnostic activities as the delivery method, not as the headline.

State a guarantee only when the operator supplies it. Write it in one sentence with the outcome, time window, and remedy, then list its conditions. Size any quantified promise to the prospect's scale and to documented comparable results, and label it as the operator's commitment, not a prediction.

## Choose the next step

Ask for the missing decision that determines fit or scope. Do not promise a free review, turnaround, or guarantee without operator authority and capacity.

## Review boundary

Use [the method](../method.md) and [shared rubric](../copy-validation-rubric.md). Keep facts,
inferences, and proposed actions distinct. Do not fabricate relevance or evidence to satisfy a
score. All send, enrichment, CRM, and scheduling actions require separate exact action approval.
