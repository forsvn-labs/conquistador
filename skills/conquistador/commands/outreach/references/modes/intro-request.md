# intro-request

Use when the sender asks a mutual contact (the connector) to introduce them to a specific person. Write two parts: a short note to the connector, and a paragraph the connector can forward without changes.

## Connector note

Name the exact person you want to meet. Give a sourced reason for choosing that person, and make one request. Do not ask for a list of names, a general "anyone you know" introduction, or several favors at once.

Give the connector an easy way to decline without a reason. Do not ask the connector to vouch for claims they cannot verify, and do not imply a closer relationship with the connector than exists.

## Forwardable paragraph

Write it in the third person so the connector can forward it as is. State the sender's name and affiliation, why the introduction is relevant to the target person, and the proposed next step. It must make sense without the rest of the thread. Do not claim that the target person has a problem; use the same signal rules as a first touch.

## Review boundary

Use [the method](../method.md), [selection evidence](../frameworks/personalization-signals.md), and [shared rubric](../copy-validation-rubric.md). The connector decides whether to forward. All send, enrichment, CRM, and scheduling actions require separate exact action approval.
