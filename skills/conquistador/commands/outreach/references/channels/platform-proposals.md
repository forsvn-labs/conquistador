# Proposals on a marketplace

Answer the actual posted requirements. Separate scope, evidence, questions, estimate assumptions, and exclusions. Do not invent completed work, platform reputation, customer names, or availability. A requested proposal may reasonably need more detail than a first-contact message.

## Pre-action checks

Use current verified marketplace rules for links, contact exchange, fees, and format. Do not move payment or contact off platform without authority and policy review. A proposal draft cannot submit a bid or bind commercial terms.

Return the complete draft with relevant gaps under [format conventions](../format-conventions.md).
Use the common claim and recipient-control gates; a channel does not waive them. Character counts
can describe the draft itself, but unverified platform limits must not be asserted as current facts.
