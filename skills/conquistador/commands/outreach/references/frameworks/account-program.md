# Plan a multi-account program

Use when outreach covers many accounts across more than one channel or sender. The other
frameworks cover the honesty of one message or sequence; this file decides which accounts get
which channels, and who owns each account.

## Score and tier the accounts

Score each account before you assign channels. Use stated, weighted factors, such as product fit,
readiness to buy from outbound, offer clarity for this account, deal size, openness to several
channels, and sector fit. Record the weights as operator-owned assumptions, not findings.

Sort scored accounts into three tiers and write which channels each tier may receive. Give capped
or costly channels (personal-network messages, calls, account-targeted ads) only to the top tier.
Give the lowest tier the scalable channel only, or nothing. Report the share of the list that gets
minimal or no touches in the period. If that share is near zero, the plan has no real tiers.

## Give each channel a role

Use the high-volume channel to learn which segments, problems, and roles respond. Feed those
findings into the targeting for capped channels. Do not raise volume on a capped channel to make
up for weak targeting.

Rank lawful warm signals above cold lists. Warm signals include engagement with the sender's
content, a resource request, a product or pricing visit identified with the visitor's consent, and
a signup that did not finish onboarding. Mention the action in a message only when the recipient
would expect the sender to know about it. Visitor-identification tools need a lawful-basis check
by the operator before use.

Add channels in this order: one channel that teaches at volume, plus content; a capped channel
once top-tier accounts exist; account-targeted ads once there are signals to build audiences from;
calls last, and only to accounts that already took an action.

## Keep one owner per account

Keep one record per account: owner, channel of first contact, stage, and last message. Send the
next touch from the same sender and channel where the prospect last replied. Never let two senders
work one account at the same time. Each sender uses their own account; never borrow, rent, or
share platform accounts to add capacity.

After a positive reply, give each follow-through state an owner and a due time: booking, the
reminder before the meeting, the out-of-office return, and "contact me later" on the date the
prospect gave. The draft does not schedule them.

## Diagnose before scaling

When the reply rate drops, check the list source, signal quality, and offer before you add inboxes,
tools, or volume. Account scores, tiers, and channel plans are drafts; sending, list uploads, CRM
writes, and ad audiences need separate exact approval under [the method](../method.md).
