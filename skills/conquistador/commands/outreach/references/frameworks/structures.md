# Check the arrangement

The structures themselves are in [the craft reference](cold-email-frameworks.md). Run these checks on
a finished draft.

- **Remove the opener.** Read the message without its first line. If the rest does not depend on
  that line, the signal is decoration: connect it to the offer or drop it.
- **Remove the close.** Read the message without its last line. If the next step is missing, add one
  clear question. A refusal or acknowledgement may correctly contain no new request.
- **Fact before inference.** A sourced observation comes first, then any implication as a question
  or an "if".
- **Scope before commitment.** A proposed service states scope and exclusions before it asks for a
  commitment.
- **Research stays research.** A research message states its purpose and asks one question about
  behavior, not approval of the product.
- **Replies answer first.** Resolve the inbound question before anything else. Do not restart the
  first-touch pitch.
- **Preview is the first line.** In email, read the subject with the first body line. A greeting or
  footer must not fill the preview.
