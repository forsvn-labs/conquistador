# Connect campaign choices to reader tasks

The campaign plan connects a business outcome to reader decisions, evidence,
content, delivery, and a review date. Every selected angle names its pillar; every
pillar names the task and product evidence it serves. This trace makes omissions
and unsupported promises visible.

## Dependency order

For a full plan run pillar-agent, angle-agent, channel-agent, timeline-agent,
launch-sequencing-agent, then critic-agent. Each role consumes the preceding output.
Use fallbacks/sequential.md without claiming independent review when the host
cannot run separate agents. A bounded parent request loads only its relevant roles.

Pillar-agent groups related reader decisions. Angle-agent proposes evidence-backed
ways to answer them using 3d-angle-framework.md. Neither requires a stage ladder,
emotion matrix, fixed theme count, or content allocation by launch phase.

Channel-agent uses actual audience access, permission, destination readiness, and
capacity. Closed candidate sets stay closed. In a full open comparison, consult
channel-strategy.md and platform-channels.md for the channel inventory. Unknown
habitat data supports a provisional test or research need, not invented density.

Timeline-agent records production, approval, delivery, and measurement dependencies.
Launch-sequencing-agent orders activation by those dependencies. Phase names describe
work and readiness; they do not prescribe how a reader must think or feel. The
critic checks traceability, feasibility, evidence, and authority.

## Platform-pack use

A method pack has last_verified: null and a separate method_updated date. Its date
does not verify current platform mechanics. Use §0 for fit questions, §1 for an
opening choice, §2 for task-verified constraints, §3 for observable measures, §5 for
a test, §6 for capacity/timing, and §7 for CTA. Do not read those sections as ranked
algorithm signals or benchmark prescriptions. Follow legibility-convention.md and
use pack_verified: none when only the method was loaded.

## Scope and handoff

Return the integrated plan with owners, constraints, evidence gaps, acceptance,
and one next decision. Individual copy or ads go to `copy`, `social`,
`outreach`, or `ads`; creative production goes to `creative`
or `video`. Budget calculations go to `budget` or
`funnel` as appropriate. Actual results go to `measure`.

Optional distribution ideas must earn a place through task fit, permissions,
economics, and measurable outcomes. A demographic label or a prescribed content
quota does not justify selecting a play. No method or internal critic can authorize
publishing, spending, outreach, or account changes.
