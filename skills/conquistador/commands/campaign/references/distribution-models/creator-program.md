# Creator program

Use this model when a campaign pays several creators with existing audiences to show the product in
their own content. It covers running the program: launch readiness, evaluation, payment checks, and
scale. Three other files own the rest; do not repeat their rules here:

- whether the niche fits creators: [creator channel fit](../../../channels/references/creator-channel-fit.md);
- creator vetting, first contact, and pay structure:
  [creator sponsorship mode](../../../outreach/references/modes/creator-sponsorship.md);
- the creator brief and placement in the creator's format: [UGC pack](../platform-intelligence/ugc.md).

## Get ready before posts go live

- Confirm the fit check passed, including the comprehension condition. If a viewer cannot understand
  the product from the clip, choose a different channel.
- Make sure search capture is live before the first post: the brand term in the app store or search
  engine leads to the right destination.
- Give each creator a unique code or link so you can track downstream outcomes. State what it cannot
  attribute, such as viewers who search the brand later.
- Model the cash gap between paying creators and collecting revenue. Set the program size so the
  operator can carry that gap.

## Diagnose each post

Read a post on both reach and conversion, then name the failure mode:

| Pattern | Likely cause | Next move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Views without conversions | The product sits on top of the content, not inside it | Rework where the product appears in the format |
| Conversions without views | The integration is too heavy and reads as an ad | Lighten the integration; give the creator more control |
| Comments ask what the product is | The product worked inside the content | Keep the pattern and brief it to others |

## Decide keep, fix, or cut for each creator

Judge each creator on the downstream outcome from their code or link, not on views. Decide keep, fix,
or cut, and note how small the sample per creator is. Early in a program, one post carries more
signal; as volume grows, wait for the agreed review period before you decide. Give most management
time to the relationships with the highest reliable reach.

When a post overperforms, break down its hook, its message, and where the product appears. Brief
that pattern to the other creators, and stop when it stops working.

## Verify each post before payment

Before you release payment, check that:

- the agreed format and integration were followed;
- the paid relationship is disclosed with the platform's tools;
- the views do not come from purchased views, bots, or engagement groups.

Withhold payment only on terms the contract states. Record each check and its result.

## Scale the operation

Automate manual work in the order of time it takes, for example contracts, then scheduling, then
performance review. Keep a human approval on contracts, payments, and anything published from the
brand account.
