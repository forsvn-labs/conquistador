# Write paid ads

Write the ads in your own judgment from the user's facts, in the customer's brand, within the
platform facts below. Load no other file for them. Then run only the facts check: remove claims
the facts do not support, keep fair inferences, and add nothing (no hedges, no source text, no
disclaimers). Return the ads in the format and size the user asked for.

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in the current specs before launch)

- **Meta (Facebook, Instagram):** feeds show about the first 125 characters of primary text before
  "See more". Headlines show about 40 characters; some mobile placements cut near 27. Descriptions
  are often hidden. One ad can carry up to 5 primary texts and 5 headlines, which Meta combines per
  viewer. Meta groups ads that look and read alike and tests them as one. Visuals: 4:5 for feeds,
  9:16 for Stories and Reels, where the interface covers the top and bottom bands and the right
  edge. Most feed video plays without sound. Ads may not assert the reader's personal attributes
  (health, finances, age, and similar). Credit, employment, housing, and social-issue ads run as
  special ad categories.
- **Google responsive search:** up to 15 headlines of 30 characters, 4 descriptions of 90, and 2 paths
  of 15. Google shows up to 3 headlines and 2 descriptions in any order and combination. Pinning
  fixes an asset to a position and cuts the combinations Google can test. Trademark rules apply to
  competitor names in ad text.
- **LinkedIn sponsored content:** about 150 characters of intro text show before "see more" (600
  maximum); headlines show about 70 characters (200 maximum).
- **TikTok in-feed:** ad text up to 100 characters; Spark Ads use the organic post's caption and need
  the creator's authorization. 9:16 full screen; keep text and faces out of the right-side buttons
  and the bottom caption band.
- **People on camera:** a customer or creator is real and consents; paid creator content carries the
  platform's disclosure.

**Campaign test** (structure, budget, decision and kill rules): use the [complete method](COMMAND.md).

Never launch, publish, spend, upload an audience or asset, or change a live account without the
user's approval for that exact action.
