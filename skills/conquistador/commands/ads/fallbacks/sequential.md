# Sequential campaign procedure

Run the roles in order in one context when the host has no separate agents. Do not claim
independent review.

1. Read the product facts, audience, offer, destination, and account data. Ask only for a missing
   fact that changes the test.
2. [Strategist](../agents/strategist.md): concepts, angles, event, structure, budget, and decision rules.
3. [Composer](../agents/composer.md): finished ads from the [craft reference](../references/ad-craft.md).
4. [Format checker](../agents/format-checker.md), then [voice auditor](../agents/voice-auditor.md).
5. [Critic](../agents/critic.md). On a failure, repair and recheck, at most two rounds.
6. Deliver the ads, creative direction, destination checks, test plan, and open checks.

Launch, publish, spend, audience or asset uploads, and live account changes each need the user's
approval for that exact action. When `campaign` calls this method, keep its offer, budget authority,
and campaign ID. Results after launch belong to `results`; planned numbers are never observed ones.
