# Meta ads for people who already know you

Retargeting ads speak to someone who has met the brand and did not act. Write the ads through the
pass-through in the [entry](../../ENTRY.md); this pack holds the audiences and the cadence.

## Choose the audiences

- Split audiences by what the person did: visited the pricing page, started a trial and stopped,
  watched a video, engaged on Instagram.
- An ad may not reveal sensitive browsing or personal attributes.
- Exclude people who already bought, unless the offer is an upsell with its own terms.

## Audiences and cadence · Starting point (Oct 2026)

| Item | Starting point |
|---|---|
| Audiences | Site visitors, video viewers, and social engagers, each split by recency |
| Recency | 30 days for a short buying cycle; 90–180 days for considered or B2B purchases |
| Budget | About 10–20% of Meta spend while acquisition is the main job |
| Frequency | Refresh when 7-day frequency passes 4 and cost per result rises |

Retargeting takes credit for some sales that would have happened anyway. Before raising its budget,
run a holdout or an off/on test when the account allows it. Uploading a customer list requires the
rights to use that data and the user's approval; so do launch and spend.
