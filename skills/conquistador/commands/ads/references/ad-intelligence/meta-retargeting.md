# Plan a Meta return-visit test

This is a campaign decision procedure. It supplies no performance benchmark or
claim of current platform verification. Record the source, date, placement, region,
and account scope of platform constraints before using them. If verification is
unavailable, deliver a draft with the affected setup decisions unresolved.

## Establish why a second contact is useful

Start with an observed prior action and an unresolved task. A website visit, an
Instagram interaction, a Facebook page interaction, and an existing purchase mean
different things. None proves purchase intent. Record the evidence for the proposed
follow-up and the information that the first contact did not supply.

## Define eligible audiences

For each proposed audience, record its source, permitted use, recency range,
estimated reachable size, exclusions, and overlap with other campaigns. Verify
availability in the applicable Meta account. Choose recency from the customer's
purchase interval and observed response by age where available. With no evidence,
label the range a test choice; do not import a default retention window.

Exclude completed actions when repeating the offer would be irrelevant. An upsell
requires its own explicit eligibility and offer. Do not upload a customer list
without authorization and the required rights to use that data.

## Draft the follow-up

Write the next useful piece of information: a demonstrated capability, an answered
question, clarified terms, or a supported reason to return. Do not assume followers
need urgency or visitors lack credibility. Tie the choice to observed questions or
state it as a hypothesis. Compare it with the current message under the same offer.

## Budget and evaluate

Record a loss ceiling, event lag, audience overlap, and a review point. Use measured
reach and frequency trends to detect repeated exposure; set an account-specific
review trigger with its rationale. Repetition alone does not prove fatigue.

Compare downstream outcomes and acquisition cost on matched reporting windows.
Retargeting can receive credit for conversions that would have happened without an
ad. Do not allocate budget in proportion to its reported CPA advantage alone.
Where feasible and authorized, propose a holdout or other incrementality test;
otherwise retain that uncertainty in the recommendation.

## Required output

Provide the audience definition, excluded actions, follow-up hypothesis, creative,
destination continuity, measurement limits, and bounded recommendation. State what
observation would justify narrowing recency, reducing delivery, refreshing the
message, or ending the test. Do not prescribe an Ads Manager click sequence from
memory or claim a setup duration.
