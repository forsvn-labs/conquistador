# Plan an Apple Ads search test

This is a campaign decision procedure. It supplies no performance benchmark or
claim of current platform verification. Record the source, date, placement, region,
and account scope of platform constraints before using them. If verification is
unavailable, deliver a draft with the affected setup decisions unresolved.

This pack covers App Store search results ads (Apple Ads, formerly Apple Search
Ads). These ads capture people who already search the App Store; they do not create
demand. Verify current campaign types, match types, and product page features in
Apple's documentation before setup.

## Structure campaigns by intent

Separate brand, category, competitor, and discovery terms into their own campaigns,
so each has its own budget and readout. Use exact match outside discovery. Use
broad match and search-match discovery only in the discovery campaign.

When a discovery term proves itself on the agreed downstream outcome, move it to an
exact-match campaign and add it as a negative keyword in discovery, so the account
does not bid against itself.

Start with conservative bids. Raise them in steps only after the stated observation
period, and record each change.

## Judge keywords on revenue

Judge each keyword on cohort revenue, payback, or another agreed downstream
outcome, not on install cost or tap rate. A keyword with a higher install cost can
be the better keyword. Record the attribution source and its lag for trial and
subscription events.

## Match the destination to the query

The product page is the ad. The first screenshots must show the outcome the searcher
wants, not a feature list; hand listing work to the `appstore` play.

Where custom product pages are available, build one for category intent and a
separate one for competitor-name queries that explains why to switch. Custom page
reports show which page converted; they do not prove incremental installs.

## Decide on brand and competitor terms

Before a creator, press, or launch push, decide on brand-term coverage. Check
whether competitors bid on the brand term and what coverage costs. If coverage is
needed, make it live before the push.

Before you bid on a competitor's name, record the applicable trademark and platform
policy. Do not use a competitor's name in assets or product pages without
permission.

## Scale sideways

Treat each additional storefront country as a test variable with its own readout.
Scale through new countries, new keywords, new product pages, and tighter
segmentation before you only raise bids.

## Handoff

Return the campaign structure, keyword lists with match types and negatives,
destination pages by query group, bid plan, downstream outcome, and keep, revise,
or stop criteria. Bids, budgets, and campaign changes require separate human
approval.
