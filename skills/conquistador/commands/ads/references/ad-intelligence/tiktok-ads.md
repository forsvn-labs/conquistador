# TikTok ads

A TikTok ad is a video first and copy second. Write the ads through the pass-through in the
[entry](../../ENTRY.md); this pack holds the platform facts and the account setup.

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026)

- In-feed ad text holds up to 100 characters. Spark Ads run an existing organic post, use its caption,
  and need the creator's authorization code and agreed usage period.
- Video is 9:16, full screen. Keep text and faces out of the right-side buttons and the bottom caption
  band.
- Viewers decide in about the first 2 seconds. Sound is on for most viewers; captions still help.
- Smart+ campaigns automate targeting, bids, and creative combinations; they still depend on
  distinct videos to test.
- Paid creator content needs the platform's commercial content disclosure.

## Account setup · Starting point (Oct 2026)

| Item | Starting point |
|---|---|
| Videos per ad group | 3–5 distinct concepts |
| Length | 15–30 seconds for direct response |
| Refresh | Every 7–14 days while spending; fatigue comes faster than on Meta |
| Spark Ads | Boost organic posts that already beat the account's median view rate |

Spark authorization, launch, and spend need the user's approval.
