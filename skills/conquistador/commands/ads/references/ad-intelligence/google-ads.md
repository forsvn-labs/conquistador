# Plan a Google Ads search test

This is a campaign decision procedure. It supplies no performance benchmark or
claim of current platform verification. Record the source, date, placement, region,
and account scope of platform constraints before using them. If verification is
unavailable, deliver a draft with the affected setup decisions unresolved.

## Scope the query and destination

This pack covers Google Ads Responsive Search Ads, or RSA. Record intent_tier as
branded, non-branded, competitor, or generic. These labels identify the comparison;
they do not assign expected CPC, conversion rate, or a mandatory bid posture.

Inspect supplied query evidence and the page the user will reach. List relevant
queries, ambiguous queries, and exclusions with reasons. Unknown demand remains
unknown. A keyword estimate is a planning input, not observed profitable traffic.

## Build a coherent asset set

For each asset, identify the offer fact or supported claim it communicates. Check
that permitted headline and description combinations remain accurate and readable.
Record required pinning and what it protects. Use the verified RSA requirements
for asset counts and lengths. Do not impose a separate minimum of distinct angles
to improve an assumed Ad Strength score.

Keep brand, category, and competitor comparisons separate where their eligibility
or measurement differs. Campaign count follows budget control and comparison needs.

Keep message match: put the target term in the keyword, an ad headline, and the
landing page's main heading, unless the page cannot truthfully carry it. When a
search term produces the agreed conversion, move it to its own exact-match ad group
or campaign, and add it as a negative where it came from.
Before using another brand in copy, record the applicable trademark and editorial
requirements and evidence of permitted use; do not infer permission from targeting.

## Establish economic and measurement limits

Record the conversion event, downstream qualification, event lag, budget ceiling,
and maximum acceptable outcome cost. Use supplied account cost and conversion
ranges to show a sensitivity calculation. If no plausible affordable test exists,
recommend zero spend and name the missing evidence or required offer change.

Quality Score, Ad Strength, Search Impression Share, and lost impression share
can prompt inspection when available. They do not determine profitability or prove
a cause. Keep their definitions and reporting scope attached to the account export.

## Handoff

Return the query scope, exclusions, complete RSA assets, combination review,
destination checks, test controls, and keep/revise/stop criteria. Smart Bidding
configuration requires current account eligibility and event information. The
brief cannot authorize bidding changes or defend branded terms regardless of cost.
