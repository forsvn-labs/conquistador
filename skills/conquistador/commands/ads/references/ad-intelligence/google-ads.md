# Google search ads

Search ads answer a query the person already typed. The craft is matching that query fast and then
giving the reason to pick you. Use the [craft reference](../ad-craft.md) for openings and proof.

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026)

- A responsive search ad holds up to 15 headlines of 30 characters, up to 4 descriptions of 90
  characters, and 2 display paths of 15 characters.
- Google shows up to 3 headlines and 2 descriptions, in any order and combination. Every asset must
  read alone, none may repeat another, and no pair may contradict.
- Pinning fixes an asset to a position and cuts the combinations Google can test. Pin only a
  required disclosure or a brand name that must always show.
- Automatically created assets and AI Max for Search can write text from the landing page. Review
  them, or turn text customization off, when claims or terms need control.
- Trademark and editorial rules apply to competitor names in ad text; bidding on a competitor term
  does not grant the right to use the name in copy.

## Write the asset set

- Give headlines different jobs: the query in plain words, the outcome, the proof, the offer or price,
  the objection answered, speed or setup, and the next step. Several headlines may share a job only
  when each says something new.
- Put the keyword or its plain meaning in at least two headlines, and in the landing page's main heading.
- Descriptions finish the argument: one carries the offer terms, one the proof, one the action.
- Local services: name the place and the response time; searchers often call from the results page.

## Account setup · Starting point (Oct 2026)

| Item | Starting point |
|---|---|
| Ad groups | One theme per ad group, one responsive search ad with 10–15 headlines and 4 descriptions |
| Match types | Exact and phrase on high-intent terms first; broad only with a conversion bid strategy and negatives |
| Bidding | Maximize conversions until about 30 conversions a month, then a target cost per acquisition |
| Search terms | Review weekly; add negatives; move a proven term to its own exact-match group |
| Brand terms | Keep in a separate campaign so brand demand does not hide non-brand cost |

Bids, budgets, and campaign changes need the user's approval.
