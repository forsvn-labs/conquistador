# Check the ad set after drafting

Draft first from [the craft reference](ad-craft.md). Then read the finished set once against this
list. Repair a failure by cutting or narrowing the line, not by adding words or disclaimers.

## The craft

| Failure | Repair |
|---|---|
| Two variations share an angle, or open on the same proof point | Give one of them a different angle and a different first line |
| The first line describes the product, not the reader's moment | Move the product to line two; open on the situation, result, or surprising fact |
| The headline is the product category or a feature list | Make it the outcome, the offer, or the next step |
| Every fact appears in every variation, or the audience definition is restated | Keep the one proof and mechanism this angle needs |
| A source, period, or method note sits in the ad copy | Move it to the operator note; keep only the qualifier truth needs |
| A disclaimer or hedge speaks in the drafting voice | Delete it; if a claim needs a hedge to be true, choose a claim that does not |
| Every variation has the same shape or the same visual | Change the opening shape and give each its own shot |

## The facts

- Every number, quote, name, price, and term in the ads appears in the user's facts. Remove anything
  that does not. Add nothing new in this step.
- A customer quote is exact and has permission to publish. A result is the result the facts state,
  with the qualifier truth needs ("the median clinic", "rated 4.8 by 2,000 buyers").
- Offer terms and the call to action match the landing page. A price or trial in the ad is the
  price or trial the buyer will see.
- No line asserts the reader's personal traits (health, finances, age, and similar). Describe the
  situation instead. Regulated categories (credit, housing, employment, health, politics) need the
  platform's current rules checked before launch.
- A creator or customer on camera is real, consents, and is disclosed as paid when paid. A scripted
  or representative scene is labeled as such in the brief.

## Character limits

Check each component against the [platform facts](ad-craft.md#platform-facts-checked-oct-2026-confirm-in-the-current-specs-before-launch)
and any limit in the user's brief. Count characters; do not estimate.

## Approval

The check is an editorial step. It does not clear policy review, and it does not authorize anything.
Never launch, publish, spend, upload an audience or asset, or change a live account without the
user's approval for that exact action, account, and payload.
