# Claim, permission, and platform review

This document defines artifact review requirements. It does not certify current
Meta, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, or LinkedIn Ads policy and is not a legal opinion.
Use supplied current policy or account evidence for the selected region, product,
placement, and account. Record the rule, source, date, and applicability.

## Evidence requirements

Every measurable claim must map to claim_list and available_proof. Check the
population, period, measurement, qualifications, and permission to publish. Remove
unsupported guarantees, fake scarcity, fabricated testimonials, and misleading
comparisons. An actual guarantee must carry its exact terms and still requires
applicable platform review; a word alone is not a universal banned-phrase test.

Verify destination price, action, eligibility, refunds, and material limitations.
Do not omit a material qualifier because the placement is short. Choose a different
claim or format if the truthful version does not fit.

## Network review record

| Network | Check before launch readiness |
|---|---|
| Meta | Applicable Advertising Standards, audience eligibility, sensitive claims, destination and placement requirements |
| Google Ads | Editorial requirements, trademark use, restricted categories, certification, final URL and asset combinations |
| TikTok Ads | Advertising Policies, Spark authorization, music and creator rights, disclosure, placement and category restrictions |
| LinkedIn Ads | Advertising Policies, targeting eligibility, professional claims, Lead Gen Form terms and data use |

Preserve these actual provider names and rules when verified. Do not convert a
creative preference, expected audience reaction, or private benchmark into policy.
Unknown requirements stay unresolved; do not invent a clearance decision.

## Review result

Return the claim and permission table with PASSED, REVISION_REQUIRED, or FORMAT_FAIL
per agents/format-checker.md. Quote or identify only the minimum rule needed to
explain a defect. Separate factual substantiation, current platform compliance,
human content acceptance, and approval of the exact external action.
