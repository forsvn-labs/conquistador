# Reels and Stories ads

For paid vertical video and images in Instagram and Facebook Reels and Stories. The copy decisions
are in the [craft reference](../ad-craft.md); feed placements and ad policy are in
[feed ads](facebook.md).

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in Meta's current specs before launch)

- Full screen, 9:16. The interface covers the top and bottom of the frame and the right edge in
  Reels. Keep text, faces, and the product clear of them; check the safe-zone overlay in the preview.
- The ad sits between organic videos, so it must look like one: a person, a hand, or the product in
  motion from the first frame. A logo or title card first reads as an ad and gets skipped.
- Reels play with sound by default, but many viewers watch muted. Write the voice-over and the
  captions so either one carries the ad alone.

Write each variation as the first seconds (what is seen, said, and on screen), the demonstration,
and the close. Organic Reels belong to `social`. Launch, spend, and uploads need the user's approval.
