# Reels and Stories ads

For paid vertical video and images in Instagram and Facebook Reels and Stories. Write the ads through
the pass-through in the [entry](../../ENTRY.md); feed placements and ad policy are in
[feed ads](facebook.md).

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in Meta's current specs before launch)

- Full screen, 9:16. The interface covers the top and bottom of the frame and the right edge in
  Reels. Keep text, faces, and the product clear of them; check the safe-zone overlay in the preview.
- The ad plays between organic videos.
- Reels play with sound by default, but many viewers watch muted.

Organic Reels belong to `social`. Launch, spend, and uploads need the user's approval.
