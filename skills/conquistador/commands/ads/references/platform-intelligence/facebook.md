# Facebook and Instagram feed ads

For paid ads in Facebook and Instagram feeds. The copy decisions are in the [craft reference](../ad-craft.md);
Stories and Reels are in [Reels and Stories ads](reels.md); account setup and kill rules are in the
[Meta pack](../ad-intelligence/meta-cold-traffic.md).

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in Meta's current specs before launch)

- With Advantage+ placements, Meta chooses where each ad appears. Supply a 4:5 version for feeds and
  a 9:16 version for Stories and Reels.
- Most feed video plays without sound. Burn in captions, and make the first frame readable as a still.
- Advantage+ creative can add text variations, crops, music, and other enhancements. Turn off any
  enhancement that could change a claim, a price, or the brand's voice.
- Copy may not assert the reader's personal attributes (health, finances, age, and similar).
  Before-and-after images are restricted for health and weight products. Credit, employment,
  housing, and social-issue ads run as special ad categories with limited targeting.

Organic posts and captions belong to `social`. Launch, spend, and uploads need the user's approval.
