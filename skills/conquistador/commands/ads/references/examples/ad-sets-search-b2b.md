# Annotated ad sets: Google search and LinkedIn

Every product, person, quote, rating, and number on this page is synthetic, invented to teach
decisions. No ad was run, and no result is reported. Each pair gives the facts, a strong set, a
weak set, and the decisions that separate them. Read for the decisions, not the shapes.

## Google search · invoicing for trade contractors

Facts: Tradebill lets contractors write an invoice on their phone at the job site. Customers pay by
card or bank transfer from a text link. Syncs with the contractor's accounting software. Half of
invoices are paid within 3 days. $25 a month after a 30-day free trial; cancel anytime. 4.8 stars
from 1,900 app store reviews. Keyword: "invoice software for contractors". Goal: trial sign-ups.

```ad-google
Product: Tradebill
Keyword: invoice software for contractors

#### Headlines
1. Invoice Software for Trades
2. Contractor Invoicing App
3. Invoice Before You Leave
4. Half Paid Within 3 Days
5. Customers Pay From a Text
6. Card or Bank Transfer
7. Free for 30 Days
8. $25 a Month, Cancel Anytime
9. 4.8 Stars From 1,900 Reviews
10. Syncs With Your Accounting

#### Descriptions
1. Write the invoice on your phone before you leave the driveway. Customers pay by text.
2. Half of Tradebill invoices are paid within 3 days, by card or bank transfer.
3. Try it free for 30 days, then $25 a month. Syncs with your accounting software.

#### Paths
1. invoicing
2. contractors

Final URL: https://example.invalid/contractors
```

```text
Weak set (headlines)

1. Invoice Software for Contractors
2. Best Invoice Software for Contractors
3. Contractor Invoice Software
4. Invoicing Software Contractors
5. #1 Invoice App for Contractors
6. Top-Rated Invoice Software
7. Easy Invoice Software
8. Invoice Software Free Trial
Description: Invoice software for contractors (based on 2026 customer data).
```

**Why the strong set wins.** Google shows any three headlines in any order, so each one has its own
job: the query in plain words (1, 2), the moment (3), the speed (4), how customers pay (5, 6), the
terms (7, 8), the proof (9), and the fit with existing tools (10). Any three still make sense
together, and none repeats another. The weak set gives eight headlines one job, so every combination
says the same thing; "best", "#1", and "top-rated" have no source in the facts. Paths and the final
URL carry the keyword, so the copy does not have to say it a fifth time.

## Google search · mobile dog grooming, local

Facts: Sudsmobile grooms dogs in a van parked at the customer's home in Denver. One dog at a time, no
cages. A full groom takes about 90 minutes. Small dogs from $75, large dogs from $110. 4.9 stars from
640 Google reviews. The online calendar shows open slots for the next 14 days. A text goes out when
the van is 20 minutes away. Keyword: "mobile dog grooming near me". Goal: bookings.

```ad-google
Product: Sudsmobile
Keyword: mobile dog grooming near me

#### Headlines
1. Mobile Dog Grooming in Denver
2. We Groom in Your Driveway
3. One Dog at a Time, No Cages
4. Small Dogs From $75
5. 4.9 Stars, 640 Google Reviews
6. See Open Slots Online
7. No Drive, No Waiting Room
8. A Groomer That Comes to You
9. Text When We're 20 Min Away

#### Descriptions
1. Our van parks at your door. Your dog gets a full groom in about 90 minutes, with no cages.
2. Small dogs from $75, large dogs from $110. Rated 4.9 from 640 Google reviews.
3. Pick a time from the open slots near you. We text when the van is 20 minutes away.

#### Paths
1. denver
2. mobile-grooming

Final URL: https://example.invalid/denver
```

```text
Weak set (descriptions)

1. Sudsmobile is a mobile dog grooming service for Denver dog owners who want grooming.
2. We offer mobile dog grooming. Results may vary depending on your dog's coat.
3. Denver's best mobile dog groomers. Call now!
```

**Why the strong set wins.** A local searcher often decides on the results page. The strong set
answers what that person is weighing: the place, the price, the stress for the dog, and the effort
of the visit. "No drive, no waiting room" names the costly part of the alternative without naming a
rival. The weak descriptions restate the query, hedge a claim nobody made, and use a superlative the
facts cannot support.

## LinkedIn · bank reconciliation for finance teams · leads on pain

Facts: Tallyroom matches bank transactions to the general ledger and flags only the lines that do
not match. Median month-end close for its customers: 4 business days, down from 9. Quote,
permission given, from a controller at a 140-person company: "We stopped working the weekend after
month-end." Offer: a free reconciliation preview; upload last month's bank export and see the match
results within one business day. Buyers: controllers and finance managers at companies with 50–500
employees. Goal: preview sign-ups.

```ad-linkedin
Product: Tallyroom

### Variation 1 · pain
Intro text: Close is on day 6 and you are still ticking bank lines against the ledger in a spreadsheet. Tallyroom matches them and flags what doesn't.
Headline: Match every bank line to the ledger without the spreadsheet
Visual: A spreadsheet with hundreds of yellow highlights collapses into a short list of three flagged lines.

### Variation 2 · proof
Intro text: "We stopped working the weekend after month-end." That's a controller whose team matches bank lines in Tallyroom.
Headline: Median close: 4 business days, down from 9
Visual: The quote on a plain card, with a calendar showing month-end weekend days left blank.

### Variation 3 · offer
Intro text: Upload last month's bank export. Within a day, see which lines would have matched without anyone touching them. Free.
Headline: Free reconciliation preview on your own data
Visual: Document-ad style preview: page one shows a sample match report; the form unlocks the reader's own.
```

```text
Weak intro text (one of three; all three open the same way)

Attention controllers and finance managers at companies with 50–500 employees! At Tallyroom,
we believe finance teams deserve a better close. Our AI-powered reconciliation platform helps
teams close in a median of 4 business days, down from 9 (internal customer data, 2026). Book
a demo today to learn more.
```

**Why the strong set wins.** A controller reads LinkedIn between tasks. The pain variation names
day 6 of close, which is where the reader is, in the first 150 characters. The proof variation lets
a peer speak and puts the number in the headline as support. The offer variation gives first: the
reader's own data, at no cost, without a sales call. The weak version spends its visible characters
on the targeting definition and a belief statement, and the number arrives after "see more" with
its source attached.

## LinkedIn · interview scheduling for recruiting teams · leads on identity

Facts: Panelpath reads interviewers' Google or Microsoft calendars and sends the candidate a link
to pick a time that works for the whole panel. Median time to book a four-person panel: one day,
down from four. 14-day free trial. Buyers: recruiting coordinators and talent leads at companies
that hire 20 or more people a year. Goal: trial starts.

```ad-linkedin
Product: Panelpath

### Variation 1 · identity
Intro text: Recruiting coordinators are the only people at work who know four calendars by heart. Panelpath reads them so you don't have to.
Headline: Book a four-person interview panel in one message
Visual: A coordinator's screen with four overlapping calendars; one link is sent; one slot turns green.

### Variation 2 · comparison
Intro text: Retire the reply-all thread that hunts for one hour when four interviewers are free. The candidate picks from times that already work.
Headline: Candidates pick a time that already works for the whole panel
Visual: A long email thread shrinks to a single calendar invite.

### Variation 3 · proof
Intro text: Median time to book a four-person interview panel with Panelpath: one day. Before, it took our customers four.
Headline: Try it free for 14 days with your Google or Microsoft calendar
Visual: Two simple bars, "before" and "after", labeled in days, with the panel's four faces shown as initials.
```

```text
Weak set (all three intros, first sentence)

1. Panelpath cuts panel scheduling from four days to one.
2. With Panelpath, panel scheduling drops from four days to one.
3. Teams using Panelpath schedule panels in one day instead of four.
```

**Why the strong set wins.** The weak set has one angle and one proof point, reworded. The strong set
gives the coordinator three different reasons: being seen (the four calendars), escaping a familiar
chore (the reply-all thread), and a result. The identity line says what the reader deals with, not
just their job title, and it is a compliment a coordinator would accept.
