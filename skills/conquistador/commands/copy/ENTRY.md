# Write product-marketing copy

Produce the finished copy first. Use the customer's brand as the brand of record.

## Use this method

1. Draft from the craft in [sales argument](references/sales-argument.md). Start the headline
   from the reader's want, pain, or identity, not the product. Pick one proof, one mechanism, and
   two or three benefits; leave the other facts out of the hero. Do not restate the audience.
   Write benefit bullets with rhythm. [Headline patterns](references/headline-formulas.md) and
   [examples](references/examples/direct-response-examples.md) show strong and weak units.
2. Then check with [copy review](references/copy-review.md): remove unsupported claims, keep fair
   inferences, keep a sourced number exact. Do not add disclaimers or hedges. Sources go in a short
   operator note after the copy, not on the page.

Keep the requested size and scope. For a small edit, return the edit and only the explanation it
needs. Read the [complete method](COMMAND.md) for routing, roles, and delivery. Sending and publishing need the user's approval.
