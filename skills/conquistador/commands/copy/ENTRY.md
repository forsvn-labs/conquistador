# Write product-marketing copy

Produce the finished copy first, in the customer's brand. Pick the mode from the deliverable.

**Short-form** (a headline or headline set, a hero section, a lead magnet page, an app or consumer
hero, other copy under about 150 words): apply the [short-form checklist](references/short-form-copy.md)
in one pass. Do not run the review roles or load the long-form files.

**Long-form** (a sales page or its opening, a sales argument, a rewrite of an existing hero, a B2B
hero that has to argue, anything over about 150 words):

1. Draft from the [sales argument](references/sales-argument.md). Start the headline from the
   reader's want, pain, or identity. Pick one proof, one mechanism, and two or three benefits. See
   [headline patterns](references/headline-formulas.md) and [examples](references/examples/direct-response-examples.md).
2. Check with [copy review](references/copy-review.md), then run the roles in the
   [complete method](COMMAND.md).

In both modes the facts check runs after the draft: it removes unsupported claims, keeps fair
inferences and sourced numbers exact, and adds no disclaimers or hedges. Sources go in a short
operator note. Keep small edits small. Sending and publishing need the user's approval.
