# Method: `social`

Turn one brief into a complete social or community artifact for one platform and market.
The reader should recognize the task, receive the promised answer and have a usable next action.

## Decision and production method

1. Establish actor, audience, goal, supported product facts and brand mode. Use the existing
   cold-start/needs-context route for material omissions. Do not fabricate experience or proof.
2. Read the matching platform-intelligence pack. §0 determines fit/defer; §1 supplies opening
   choices; §2 lists task-local format checks; §3 defines observable measures; §4 checks
   anti-patterns; §5 specifies a bounded test; §6 uses operator capacity for timing; §7 defines
   the CTA path; §8 records unknowns; §9 records method changes.
3. Produce one to three opening variants that change a meaningful entry point, with a complete
   body and CTA. Use `hook-archetypes.md` for task, decision, bounded-evidence and answer choices.
   A label is descriptive. It does not establish quality or a platform preference.
4. Count each publication unit, inspect supplied constraints and preview evidence, and mark
   missing checks. A method_updated date identifies the method, not verified platform facts.
5. Check reading continuity and production completeness. Social needs the caption/body, variants,
   media dependencies and action path. Launch needs listing, gallery, maker/anchor narrative,
   notify and cross-post drafts, reply ownership and follow-up. Community work adds community
   fit, submission lane, disclosure and flair checks when applicable.
6. Score with the five dimensions in rubric.md. Compare with a weakened draft to check whether
   the review detects missing task relevance, answer, evidence and action. Scores are editorial
   conventions, not predictions. Known hard failures override totals.

The copywriter, format/guard checker and critic retain separate responsibilities. Sequential
fallback uses the same contracts in one context. Baseline format revision allows one bounce;
the next unresolved defect returns FORMAT_FAIL, or GUARD_FAIL for launch. Critical-gates owns
mode-specific limits. No critic rewrite loop is implied.

## Scope and handoff

Use one artifact per platform and market so its audience, account constraints, preview and
production dependencies stay explicit. Paid ads route to `ads`; on-site copy to
`copy`; one-to-one outreach to `outreach`; complete video production to `video`.
This skill owns social captions and launch media instructions, not the finished video.

The local artifact path is `.forsvn/artifacts/mkt/social/[platform]-[date]-[slug].md` when that
store exists; deliver inline otherwise. Keep the frontmatter and ordered body contract in
format-conventions.md. Legibility identifies the loaded method and unknowns. Why this works
identifies supplied product foundations or uses Absent. Neither block belongs in native copy.

Optional terminal editorial-polish or `vietnamese` follows pass/done_with_concerns only.
Preserve variants and baseline critic scores; record Body/CTA changes and recheck affected
constraints before publication. Vietnamese-market work retains the existing register handoff.
A missing optional sibling never justifies claiming that a separate polish pass ran.

The measurement handoff carries the selected variable, outcome, denominator, comparison, window,
stop rule and owner. Missing observations yield a test plan. Keep all synthetic examples labeled.
Publishing, sending and scheduling remain separate operator-controlled actions.

## Compatibility

Preserve critic_score, critic_verdict, completion statuses, polish_chain_applied and the required
body section order. The fourth rubric dimension now checks reading continuity. The per-variant
measure label is Observable measure targeted. `pack_verified` remains none for draft methods;
`platform_intel_version` identifies method_updated. Dependent integrations must read those meanings.
