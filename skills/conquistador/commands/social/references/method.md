# Check a social post after drafting

Draft first from [the entry](../ENTRY.md) and the channel file. Then run this check on the finished
draft. The check decides whether each line is true and whether the post may go out. It does not
predict reach or score quality.

## After drafting

1. **Facts.** Compare every name, number, date, price, result, and product capability with what the
   user supplied. Narrow or delete anything unsupported, and add nothing. Do not swap a deleted
   claim for a vaguer one.
2. **First person.** An experience, feeling, or story told in the first person must come from the
   person posting. If the post needs one you do not have, ask for it or write the post without it.
   A risk the facts already imply, such as cancellations after a price rise, may be stated as the
   decision's risk.
3. **Quotes and customers.** Use a customer's or employee's quote, name, logo, or result only with
   the permission the user supplied. Quote words exactly. Without consent, leave the person out.
4. **Limits.** Count each unit against the channel's platform facts: title, tagline, each thread
   post, caption, hashtags. Check the named community's own rules.
5. **Reread as the reader.** Read the edited post once more against the channel craft. A fix must
   not add a disclaimer, a source line, or words the reader does not need. Source and method notes
   belong in the operator note, not the post.

## Deliver

Return the post ready to paste. Then add a short operator note: facts you removed, limits you
checked, open questions, and what the user must confirm (community rules, consent, links).

In edit mode, keep the author's wording and change only what a check or the user's request needs.
Name each change.

Nothing is posted, scheduled, published, or sent, no vote or reply is made as the account, and no
account setting changes until the user approves the exact text and the action. A draft is not a
published post.
