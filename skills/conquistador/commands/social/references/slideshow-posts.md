# Slideshow posts

Use for a photo slideshow or swipeable image post on TikTok, Instagram, or a similar feed. A
slideshow is a separate format from video: it is read one still at a time, at the viewer's pace.
For carousel sizes and assembly, use `creative/references/asset-types.md`. Platform limits come from
the channel file in `channels/`.

## Find a proven structure

1. Search niche keywords on the target platform. Find recent slideshows with strong engagement and
   active comments. Record the post date, the account, and what the comments ask about.
2. Adapt the structure of what you find. Never copy its images, text, or sound choice wholesale.
3. Pick one structure and name it in the draft:
   - a ranked list;
   - a tutorial that teaches real, usable steps;
   - before and after;
   - slides cut to a sound's beats or lyrics;
   - a current trend template adapted to the niche.

## Draft the slides

- Put the hook on slide 1 in a plain pattern, such as "reasons why", "stop doing X if you want Y",
  or "things I wish I knew before". Write two or three alternative hooks for testing.
- Put one idea on each slide. A swiping viewer must get it at a glance, so write no paragraphs.
- Use one visual style across all slides. Fill the frame, and use only images you have the right
  to use.
- Give value first. Introduce the product late, as part of the answer, never on slide 1. In a
  ranked list, do not put your own product at the top.
- Make the product slide recognizable: a real screenshot plus the store listing with icon and name.
- Write the call to action in first person only when it is true for the account owner. When a niche
  theme account promotes its owner's product, disclose the ownership as the `social` disclosure
  rules require. A profile note alone may not meet them.

## Learn from results

- Judge a slideshow by comments that ask for the product name and by installs or sign-ups traced
  to it, not by views.
- After a post works, publish variations of the same hook and topic before you start a new
  concept. Stop when the variations stop working.
- Slide count, call-to-action position, and posting volume are test variables. Set them per niche
  and record them with each post; do not treat any default as a target.
