# Social copy critic rubric

Score five dimensions from 0 to 10. Scores are local review conventions, not forecasts or
platform benchmarks. Cite the draft passage and the check for every score. Pack §1 offers
opening choices; §2 identifies constraints to verify for this task; §3 defines observable measures.

| Dimension | 8–10 | 5–7 | 1–4 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook scroll-stop strength | Opening names a relevant task or decision and the body delivers its supported promise | Relevant promise with avoidable setup or incomplete scope | Generic promise, weak audience fit or unsupported implication | No usable opening or promise contradicts the body |
| Char/word limit compliance | Counts are accurate and task-local constraints are evidenced for the selected account and format | Counts are accurate; a noncritical constraint or preview check is explicitly pending | Counts or required constraint sources are missing or contradictory | A supplied hard constraint is violated |
| CTA placement vs truncation | Reader can find and complete the declared action in the supplied preview or artifact; destination and offer agree | Path is clear in copy but preview or destination check is pending | Ambiguous action, competing requests or missing delivery plan | Action cannot be completed or asks for manipulation |
| Reading continuity | Each sentence, slide or scene advances the promised answer; transitions preserve meaning | Answer is complete with a removable repetition or detour | Repeated setup, unexplained jumps or distracting formatting impede the answer | Body never delivers the promised answer |
| Format compliance | All requested components, media instructions, owners and handoff dependencies are usable | Correct format with a noncritical production detail pending | Missing components or unclear production ownership | Wrong deliverable format or no feasible production path |

For a score within a band, explain the specific remaining defect. Do not reward a named hook,
interrupt count, assumed posting time, external-link placement theory or presumed algorithm signal.
Apply CTA checks to every requested format; no platform gets a default ten. A deliberate
no-CTA awareness piece must name the reader outcome and explain how it is delivered.

## Tone and reshare checks

Run both checks before scoring and cite each failure under the dimension it weakens.

- **Earned tone.** Each emotional claim comes from the author's direct experience or supplied
  evidence. The author would say it the same way to a peer in person. The intensity matches the
  stakes. The post helps the reader decide or act. Cut or rewrite any claim that fails.
- **Reshare value.** Name what a reader shows their own network by resharing the post, such as
  competence, being early or generosity. If nothing applies, record that the post is not built for
  reshares. Do not add bait to fix it.

## Verdict

1. SKILL stops for Unverified first-person or Internal jargon in native copy force `fail`.
2. A known hard constraint violation or unusable required action forces `fail`.
3. Total below 25 is `fail`.
4. Total 25–34, any dimension below 4, or a material task-local verification gap is `done_with_concerns`.
5. Otherwise total at least 35 is `pass`.

Preserve the 50-point total and status tokens. A passed draft is not publication approval or
live verification. Missing facts stay unknown. Format failures retain the bounded revision path.

## Discrimination test

Compare the submission with a deliberately weakened version that removes the concrete task,
supporting answer and usable action. Score both honestly with the same constraints. State which
checks detect the loss. Do not force an estimated score to fit a target band. If the weak version
passes or loses no relevant points, report `RUBRIC INTEGRITY WARNING` and inspect those checks.
Label both simulated comparisons and review scores as synthetic evaluation, not audience results.
