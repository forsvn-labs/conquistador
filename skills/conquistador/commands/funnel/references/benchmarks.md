# Evaluate a proposed benchmark

This installed reference supplies a comparison procedure, not a table of claimed industry results. Use actual authorized measurements or a cited primary source supplied or verified for the task. When neither exists, leave the comparison unavailable.

## Record the source

Capture the metric definition, population, period, region, sample, collection method, and publication date. Distinguish observed measurements from a vendor claim, forecast, or illustrative example. Preserve limitations and the source location.

## Check comparability

Compare numerator, denominator, time window, acquisition mix, product maturity, and inclusion rules with the user's metric. A similarly named metric can describe a different event. Explain any mismatch before using the number in a decision.

Check whether the comparison omits failed users, closed businesses, refunds, or incomplete observation windows. Avoid generalizing from a selected success story. If the source cannot be inspected, record it as unverified rather than authoritative.

## Use the comparison

Prefer the user's comparable historical baseline for target-setting. Use external observations to generate a question or scenario, not a mandatory target. Tie the proposed change to an initiative and evidence of its likely effect.

Separate baseline, assumed improvement, resulting forecast, and desired target. Include a sensitivity range derived from stated assumptions. Do not label those assumptions industry averages or promise a fixed lift based on maturity, channel, or a redesign.

## Output

Return source, metric definition, comparability, uncertainty, permitted use, and the decision it informs. Unavailable evidence produces an unknown field or an evidence request, never a fabricated benchmark.

## Synthetic example

A comparison says 20 of 100 accounts completed a task within a week. The user's denominator is all visitors in a day. Those figures cannot support a direct conversion comparison. First align the cohort and event definitions. The numbers in this example are constructed and establish no performance standard.
