# Check physical product commitments

Use when a proposal involves inventory, fulfillment, returns, or physical production.

## Model an order

List the realized selling price, discounts, product cost, payment fees, packaging, shipping subsidy, expected return handling, and other variable costs. Keep estimates separate from paid invoices. Calculate contribution before acquisition and after the proposed acquisition cost. State the currency and period.

Model cash timing separately. A profitable order may still require inventory payment before the business receives cash. Include supplier deposits, minimum quantities, delivery uncertainty, and stock that may not sell.

## Test the binding constraint

Identify whether the next decision is about demand, supply reliability, fulfillment capacity, or acquisition economics. Choose a test that addresses that constraint. Do not solve an inventory risk by assuming repeat purchases that have not been observed.

Compare a smaller batch, a clearly disclosed preorder, and a full order only when each is operationally feasible. Document the obligations and refund terms of any preorder. Drafting an offer does not authorize accepting payment.

## Review sensitivity

Show how the decision changes if returns, delivery cost, or sell-through differ from the estimate. Use ranges supplied by the user or labeled assumptions; do not borrow another brand's revenue or margin as a benchmark.

## Output

Return the order economics, cash exposure, uncertain inputs, and a bounded purchasing or demand test. Include supplier and customer obligations, the approval owner, and a stop condition.

## Example, synthetic

An accessory has positive margin before shipping. A remote-delivery surcharge may remove that margin for one region. The next useful action is to verify the regional delivery quote and revise the offered regions, rather than assume advertising efficiency will cover the gap.

## Limit

This check does not certify accounting treatment, tax, product safety, or legal selling terms. Route those unresolved requirements to the responsible specialist.
