# LinkedIn video research and test method

Original Conquistador guidance for collecting evidence and proposing an account-scoped test. This file contains no verified ranking model or performance benchmark. Technical limits must come from evidence for the actual publishing route.

## Fit and defer decision

This destination is opt-in through `--platforms linkedin` or `--all`. Select it when the brief identifies a professional task and there is evidence the intended people can encounter the account. Job titles or B2B labels alone do not establish fit.

Defer a recommendation when the audience task, asset rights or destination is unknown. An incomplete public sample can still supply questions to investigate, but it cannot establish account performance or platform-wide behavior.

## Inspection worksheet

For each inspected item, retain its URL, observation date, account, market, publication date if visible, and access limitations. Describe what was actually inspected. A search snippet or thumbnail does not establish the video sequence or soundtrack.

| Decision | Evidence to capture | Candidate test |
|---|---|---|
| Work context | Identify the job, constraint and decision shown in the video. Check that the opening supplies enough context for someone outside the team. | Compare a problem statement with a concrete work example. Keep the proposed decision and supporting evidence consistent. |
| Demonstration legibility | Inspect screen recordings, charts and captions at the intended display size. Check that claims remain understandable without audio. | Enlarge the decisive detail or divide the demonstration into comprehensible steps. Choose caption delivery through an actual preview, not a claimed engagement lift. |
| Post and video agreement | Compare the accompanying text with the evidence in the video. Capture contradictions, missing conditions and unsupported claims. | Revise the text to state the same bounded conclusion. Observe whether responses address the intended work problem. |
| Response quality | Separate relevant questions or requests from raw reaction counts. Record the visible response context without inferring professional authority. | Offer a useful next step appropriate to the task. Compare qualified follow-up and moderation cost, not a presumed reply ranking advantage. |

## Format and production checks

Before handing off a production brief, record the selected upload route, account capabilities, aspect ratio, duration, file constraints, caption treatment, cover crop and destination. Mark unknown values explicitly. Verify changing limits using current first-party documentation or an authorized account check when the task permits it. This method is not a substitute for that evidence.

Preview the intended export at its viewing size. Check subject visibility, text contrast, caption timing, control overlap and intelligibility with sound off. Retain qualifications needed to understand the claim. Reframe an asset only when the new crop preserves the demonstration; otherwise produce a suitable shot.

Record footage, music and excerpt permissions separately from format checks. Credit does not itself grant reuse rights. Do not infer a reach penalty from watermarks or a benefit from original audio; establish permission and presentation quality first.

## Action route

Inspect the exact post-to-destination route, including access or sign-in requirements. Do not move a link into comments on the assumption that this improves distribution.

Match the call to action to the value delivered. State what the viewer receives and what effort or access is required. A request for a save, share or reply needs a practical purpose beyond raising a visible count.

## Test contract and interpretation

Write the viewer task, the uncertain decision, the proposed change and the comparison before production. Hold other material choices stable where feasible. Record differences in audience, distribution, publication conditions and asset age that prevent a fair comparison.

Select an observable outcome that answers the decision. Record the metric definition, numerator, denominator, observation window and data access. Public views and reactions do not reveal private retention, sales or distribution logic. Use `metric_unobserved` when a required value is unavailable.

When authorized account evidence exists, compare against a relevant account baseline. Choose the review point and acceptable tradeoffs from the task's cost, risk and available evidence. Do not substitute a universal completion threshold, engagement lift or reach multiplier. If evidence cannot distinguish the options, report the uncertainty and the next observation needed.

## Research handoff

Provide the selected concept, evidence references, opening and explanation sequence, captions, permitted audio, export checks, action route, outcome measure and unresolved questions. Label each statement as an inspected observation, a verified technical constraint or a proposed test. Source pairs show differences in the captured sample; they do not prove which treatment caused a result.

Preserve `mechanics_sources_verified[]` for genuinely checked technical claims. Each entry needs the exact source URL, relevant claim and available source date. Record the inspection date separately; an unknown update date remains unknown. Do not add this method file as a verified platform source or invent an entry when no verification occurred.

## Completion checks

- The platform choice follows the viewer task and available account evidence.
- The brief can be produced and previewed with the stated assets and permissions.
- Technical claims identify their evidence and scope; missing checks stay visible.
- The proposed test defines a comparison and a measurable decision.
- Sample observations retain URLs, context and limits without a causal ranking claim.
