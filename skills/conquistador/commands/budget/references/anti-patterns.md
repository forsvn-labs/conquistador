# Allocation errors to avoid

Do not fill missing account results with a channel benchmark. Keep historical average cost distinct from marginal response.

Do not impose a platform spending floor, saturation multiplier, or concentration cap without a supplied or explicitly agreed basis. A labeled assumption is acceptable for a scenario; it is not an observed fact.

Do not split the budget into unusable tests merely to show channel diversity. Explain the observation each test can produce and the actual commitment it requires.

Do not spend the reserve twice, ignore delivery capacity, or treat a forecast as cash already earned. Reconcile amounts and state timing.

Do not end with a data request alone. Recommend a bounded allocation, a learning option, or holding funds, with the evidence that would change the choice.

Do not purchase media or change live accounts because an internal reviewer passed the proposal. Spending authority remains human-owned.
