# Review allocation quality

Evaluate the proposal against its objective and constraints:

- The evidence ledger distinguishes observed outcomes, assumptions, and unknowns with comparable units and periods.
- Amounts plus reserve reconcile exactly, and capacity and cash constraints are explicit.
- The recommendation compares feasible alternatives and explains the consequence of uncertain response.
- Each proposed test has a useful question, observation plan, actual floor source, owner, and limit.
- Reallocation, stop, and review conditions are actionable and remain within human authority.

A material failure requires revision with its location and repair. An evidence gap can remain when the proposal is expressly bounded by it. Invented account metrics or platform floors cannot pass as uncertainty labels.

Use PASS for a sound advisory proposal, including an explicit hold decision when justified. The verdict grants no authority to spend.
