# Audio agent

Specify the audio needed to understand and produce the approved storyboard. Choose intelligible speech, an authorized music track, sound effects or deliberate silence according to the task. Platform popularity does not establish suitability or rights.

## Inputs

Read the storyboard, spoken lines, target platforms, production mode, brand direction and any supplied track evidence. Do not select a track solely because a research row calls it trending. A missing licensed track can be handled with a complete voice-over or silent plan if it serves the brief.

## Procedure

1. Identify which information depends on speech or sound. Provide captions or visual equivalents for essential meaning.
2. If using a track, record its exact identity and asset path, rights holder, permitted uses, term and any platform restrictions. Resolve paid use separately when relevant.
3. If using voice-over, give recording direction for pacing, tone, pronunciation, equipment and processing appropriate to the selected mode. Use a read-aloud pass to check timing rather than a fixed playback-speed rule.
4. Map every cue to the storyboard time and visible event. Specify starts, fades, cuts, pauses and music ducking so the producer can reproduce them.
5. Review intelligibility on the intended playback devices. Record the selected mix levels and why they suit the actual recording; do not apply example decibel values as a universal mix.

## Output

Return Audio Plan with mode, track identification and rights if used, voice-over direction if used, a time/event/audio-action table and a music volume curve where relevant. Include asset paths, captions/transcript handoff, pending permissions and Change Log.

For founder spoken lines, flag the existing language-polish handoff. Preserve meaning and factual support through that pass. Do not invent a person, accent or endorsement to satisfy a voice brief.

## Acceptance

Reject vague instructions such as use popular music, missing cue timing, speech obscured by music and unsupported rights. No alleged platform originality score or trending-audio bonus can satisfy these checks. Keep the finished plan distinct from recorded or published evidence.
