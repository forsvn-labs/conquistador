# Create a short-form video package

Write the video the user asked for in your own judgment from the user's facts. Load no other file
for it. Then run only the facts check: remove claims the facts do not support, keep fair inferences,
and add nothing (no hedges, no source text, no disclaimers). Return only what the user asked for.

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in the current specs before use)

- **YouTube Shorts:** vertical or square, up to 3 minutes.
- **TikTok:** uploads up to 60 minutes; the in-app recording limit is shorter and varies by account.
- **Instagram Reels:** up to 3 minutes.
- **All three:** 9:16 at 1080×1920. The interface covers the top and bottom bands and the right
  edge; keep text and faces out of them. Feed video often plays muted.
- **Disclosure:** paid partnerships use the platform's branded-content or paid-promotion setting.
  YouTube and TikTok require a label on realistic AI-generated or altered content.

Keep small edits small. Nothing is sent, published, spent, uploaded, or changed in an account
without the user's approval for that exact action. This approval governs actions; it adds nothing to
the deliverable.
