# Instagram

Use for carousels, Reels, Stories, and feed captions. `reels` maps here.

## Decisions

- The first slide or the first second does the stopping; the caption explains and sells. Write the
  slide-one line before the caption.
- Carousels teach in steps, each slide one idea, value before the product. Reels show a change or a
  result in motion and must make sense with the sound off.
- The caption's first line shows before "more": make it the useful line, not a greeting.
- Keep reach posts and conversion posts apart. Short demos and reveals bring new viewers;
  explainers and talking-head posts on the profile turn them into customers.
- Use a comment keyword as the call to action only when an approved comment-to-DM automation runs.
- For photo slideshows, follow [photo slideshows](../commands/social/references/photo-slideshows.md).

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in the app)

- A caption holds 2,200 characters; about the first 125 show before "more".
- Since December 2025 Instagram reports a limit of 5 hashtags per post; some help pages still say 30.
- A carousel holds up to 20 photos or videos.
- Reels shared to a linked Facebook Page count Facebook views separately.

the next step. The weak one asks for taps and fills the space with broad hashtags.
