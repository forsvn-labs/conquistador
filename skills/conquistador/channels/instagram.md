# Instagram channel

Use for Reels, carousels, Stories, or feed posts. Verify current format, safe-zone, audio, link, and
disclosure requirements when they affect the work.

- Choose one format for the communication job; do not force the same asset into every surface.
- Make the opening frame and sound-off message understandable before asking for attention elsewhere.
- Use carousels for progressive explanation and Reels for a demonstrated change or story in motion.
- Ground visuals in real product behavior, customer evidence, or clearly labeled representation.
- Treat saves, shares, qualified profile actions, and downstream behavior as different signals.
- Keep reach content and conversion content separate. Short demo or reveal posts bring new viewers;
  explainers, updates, and talking-head posts on the profile convert them. Do not make one asset do both.
- Use a comment keyword as the call to action only when the account runs an approved comment-to-DM
  automation. Verify the platform's current automation rules first.
- Check whether Reels also post to the linked Facebook Page. Report Facebook views as a separate audience.
- For photo slideshows, follow [photo slideshows](../commands/social/references/photo-slideshows.md).

Deliver native copy, frame/shot sequence, accessibility text, reply plan, and a truthful publication
boundary. `reels` maps here; it is not a separate capability.
