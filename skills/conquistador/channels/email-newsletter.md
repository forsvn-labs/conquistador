# Email newsletter channel

Use for an owned newsletter issue, launch email, editorial sequence, or subscriber update. The reader
asked for these emails. Each issue earns the next open.

## Write the issue

- **One issue, one job.** Lead with a single idea, story, or useful find the reader can use this
  week. Cut the warm-up paragraph.
- **Subject and preview are one unit.** The subject names the topic or the payoff; the preview adds
  the detail. The body must pay off what the subject promised.
- **Put the value in the email.** Link out for depth, not for the point. Use one main action.
- **Pick a repeatable format:** one deep idea; a short list of finds, each with one line on why it
  matters; a teardown of one example; a build update with a real number; a reader question answered.
- **One voice.** Write as one person to one reader, on a predictable day.

## Welcome and growth

Send the welcome email at once. Say what the reader will get and how often, link the best past
issue, and ask one question they can answer by reply. Grow from owned surfaces first: a signup
form on every post and page, a referral reward for subscribers who share, and recommendation swaps
with newsletters whose readers overlap. Buy subscribers only after you know the cost per engaged
subscriber.

## Starting points (Oct 2026)

Replace these with the sender's own data.

| Item | Starting point |
|---|---|
| Cadence | Weekly or every two weeks, on a fixed day |
| Subject | Under 50 characters, with the topic first |
| Inactive subscribers | After about 90 days with no clicks or replies, send one re-permission email, then suppress |
| Spam complaint rate | Under 0.1% |

## Measure

Evaluate delivery, qualified clicks and replies, downstream behavior, complaints, and unsubscribes by
segment, not opens alone. Privacy features in some mail apps record opens that no person made.

## Send boundary

Keep claims, personalization, sender identity, a physical address, and a working one-click
unsubscribe truthful in every issue. Verify current sending, consent, and regional requirements when
material. Deliver the subject and preview, complete body, segment or suppression notes, reply
handling, and measurement. Scheduling and sending need the user's approval for the exact issue,
list, and time.
