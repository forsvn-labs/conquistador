# Email newsletter channel

Use for an owned newsletter issue, launch email, editorial sequence, or subscriber update. Verify
current sending, consent, identity, and jurisdictional requirements when material.

- Preserve the sender-recipient relationship: why this person receives the message and what was
  promised at signup.
- Make subject, preview, opening, body, and action one coherent argument.
- Deliver useful value in the email; do not hide the entire point behind a click.
- Keep claims, personalization, sender identity, physical/contact details, and opt-out truthful.
- Evaluate delivery, qualified clicks/replies, downstream behavior, complaints, and unsubscribes by
  segment—not opens alone.

Deliver subject/preheader, complete body, segment or suppression notes, reply handling, measurement,
and the explicit human-owned send boundary.
