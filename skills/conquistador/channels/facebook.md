# Facebook

Use for Page posts and posts in Facebook groups.

## Decisions

- In a group you are a member first. Share what worked in your own business, specifically enough
  that someone can copy it tomorrow. Mention your product once, as something you made from that
  experience, and only where the group's rules allow it.
- On a Page, write for the people who already know you: a change, a person behind the business, an
  answer to a question customers keep asking.
- Open on a moment the reader knows from their own day, such as a question customers ask at the
  counter.
- End with a question members can answer from experience. Reply to the answers.

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in the group)

- Each group sets its own rules, often pinned or under "About"; many ban promotion or links outside
  one weekly thread. Admins can remove posts and members.
- A post holds 63,206 characters, but the feed shows only the first few lines before "See more".

## Annotated pairs

The people and products are synthetic. Nothing here was posted.

Post in a group of independent bakery owners; group allows a link if the post is useful.

```strong
"Does this have nuts in it?" was the question we heard most at our counter last year, so we tried something.

We put a small card next to each tray, listing which of the 14 major allergens that bake contains. Questions at the till dropped, the Saturday queue moved faster, and two regulars told us it was why they kept coming.

The cards don't need to be fancy. A folded index card and a marker works. The one rule: update the card the same morning the recipe changes.

I've turned our template into a free printable, no sign-up needed: https://crumbandrye.example/allergen-cards

Has anyone found a neater way to show allergens without cluttering the display?
```

```weak
Hi all! Admin please delete if not allowed 🙏 I just launched BakeLabel, the #1 allergen labelling software for bakeries! First 50 to sign up get 20% off. Comment LABEL and I'll DM you!
```

**Why:** The strong post gives a fix anyone in the group can use without buying anything and offers
the template as a by-product. The weak one is an ad that knows it breaks the rules.
