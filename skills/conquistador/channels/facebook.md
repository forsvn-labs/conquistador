# Facebook channel

Use for a Page post, Group contribution, founder post, or focused paid/organic community execution.
Verify current placement, format, group, and advertising rules when material.

- Match the specific Page, Group, or audience context; do not treat Facebook as one homogeneous feed.
- Lead with a recognizable situation, useful artifact, or concrete change and make the post valuable
  without requiring a click.
- Use media that demonstrates the mechanism and remains legible in-feed and on mobile.
- Respect group self-promotion rules, disclosure, privacy, and audience expectations.
- Separate organic community response from paid delivery and attributed downstream behavior.

Deliver ready copy, media direction, disclosure, reply plan, and the human-owned posting or campaign
action.
