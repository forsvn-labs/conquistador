# Hacker News channel

Use for a Show HN launch, substantive discussion, or evidence-led founder post. Verify current Show HN
and site guidance before advising on title, submission, repost, or participation.

- Lead with something people can inspect, use, or learn from; a landing page alone is weak evidence.
- Use a plain descriptive title and explain the technical or product mechanism without marketing gloss.
- Disclose affiliation and answer skeptical questions directly, including limitations and alternatives.
- Treat comments as product evidence, not a debate to win. Do not coordinate votes or manufacture
  engagement.
- Measure qualified use, feedback, and follow-through rather than front-page position alone.

Deliver the submission title/link choice, optional founder context, likely reply bank, rule check, and
a no-submit recommendation when the artifact is not ready.
