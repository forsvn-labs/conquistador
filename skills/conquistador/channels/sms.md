# SMS and WhatsApp channel

Use for marketing or service text messages, SMS or MMS programs, and WhatsApp Business messages.
Do the work in your own judgment from the user's facts. These are dated platform and legal facts,
not drafting rules.

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in the current rules before use)

- **US consent:** marketing texts sent with an automated system need the recipient's prior express
  written consent. Consent cannot be a condition of purchase. The opt-in must name the sender and
  what messages the person will get.
- **US opt-out:** "stop", "quit", "end", "revoke", "opt out", "cancel", and "unsubscribe" each revoke
  consent. Since 11 April 2025, the FCC requires revocation to be honored within 10 business days
  at most, and sooner when practicable. A later FCC order may let a sender name exclusive opt-out
  methods; confirm its status before relying on it. Send at most one confirmation after an opt-out.
- **US quiet hours:** the federal window is 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. in the recipient's local time. Florida,
  Oklahoma, Washington, and Oregon end at 8 p.m. Schedule inside the narrowest window that applies.
- **US sender registration:** application-to-person texts from a 10-digit US number (10DLC) need
  brand and campaign registration before the first send. Carriers block unregistered traffic.
  Short codes and toll-free numbers have their own approval processes.
- **WhatsApp Business:** outside the 24-hour customer service window that opens when a person
  messages the business, only approved templates can be sent. Templates are marketing, utility, or
  authentication. Meta bills per delivered template message since 1 July 2025. Marketing templates
  to US (+1) numbers have been paused since April 2025.
- **Other markets:** consent rules differ by country, for example in Canada, the EU, and the UK.
  Check the recipient's country before you plan a send.

Keep small edits small. Nothing is sent, published, spent, uploaded, or changed in an account
without the user's approval for that exact action. This approval governs actions; it adds nothing to
the deliverable.
