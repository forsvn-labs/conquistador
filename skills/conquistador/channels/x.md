# X

Use for posts, threads, launch posts, and replies on X.

## Decisions

- The first post is the whole pitch for most readers. It must stand alone: the result, the news, or
  the stakes, in plain words.
- A thread earns its length with a story the reader wants the end of: the decision and what it
  risked, what was done, what happened, what it cost, what you would repeat. Each post makes one
  move and reads on its own if quoted.
- A launch post says what it is, who made it, and why to try it today. The link and offer can go
  in a reply.
- End a thread on a question peers can answer from experience, and answer the early replies.

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in the composer)

- A post holds 280 characters on a standard account and up to 25,000 with Premium.
- A link counts as 23 characters, whatever its length.
- In July 2026 X's product lead said link posts no longer lose reach. Check your own data.
- Use one hashtag or none.

Thread on removing a free plan; goal: founders reply.

```strong
We deleted our free plan in May. Signups fell by half, and new paying customers went from 60 a month to 95.

Here is what happened and what I would do differently.
---
Loomtag prints shipping and care labels for small online craft shops. The free plan allowed 20 labels a month.

Most free users never printed a 21st label. They were not trying us out. They were using us, for free, with no reason to pay.
---
So we replaced it with a 14-day trial of the full product, no card needed.

Signups went from about 900 a month to 450. The people who did sign up tried the whole product, and more of them paid.
---
It had a cost. Support tickets doubled for two weeks. People who had used the free plan for a year were angry, and some were right to be, because we told them only 14 days ahead.
---
Next time I would give 60 days' notice and a discount on the first paid year to anyone already on the free plan.

If you have removed a free plan, how much notice did you give?
```

```weak
🧵 Freemium is DEAD. Every founder should kill their free plan. 👇
---
We killed ours and revenue EXPLODED 🚀 The lesson? Charge from day one. Follow for more!
```

**Why:** The strong thread opens on the decision and both results, admits the cost, and says what
to repeat. The weak one claims a universal rule, swaps numbers for "exploded", and begs for follows.
