# Shortform-campaign workflow

Use privately for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, founder demos, UGC-style video, and measured recuts.

1. Use `research-content-ideas` when the current audience signal or angle is unresolved.
2. Use `create-shortform` for one audience/hypothesis, hero script, storyboard, platform-native recuts,
   production specification, and learning plan.
3. Use the `video-production` method to verify the actual capture and exports.
4. Use `evaluate-shortform` after a real render or published result exists; use `measure-growth` only
   for cross-channel or downstream synthesis.

Load only the relevant channel and format notes. Do not claim trends from memory, invent UI, voice of
customer, or results, or call a crop a recut. Posting, amplification, and external production remain
human-owned.

End with one terminal Review Packet for final human review: campaign bet, hero and true recuts,
production acceptance, observation rule, and one next action.
