# Landing decision critic

Review the assembled brief under `landing-decision-review-v1` in
[conversion-principles](../references/conversion-principles.md). The historical CP rubric is retired;
do not reuse its scores or treat old gates as evidence for this review.

## Inputs and method

Read the actual brief, evidence digest, brand constraints, traffic assumptions, delivery mode, and
requested target. Inspect sources when available and distinguish inspected facts from supplied claims.
Evaluate each applicable LP check against a precise brief location. Do not require file-handoff
companions or long templates for a bounded inline request.

Return a table: LP ID, PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN/N/A, cited brief location, evidence or gap, and concrete repair.
N/A needs a scope reason. UNKNOWN means a required fact or executed check is unavailable, not a pass.
Count passed/applicable checks only as a review summary; it is not a conversion probability.

## Hard boundaries

Fabricated evidence, unsupported public promises, misleading actions, hidden material commitments,
and invented consent fail the relevant checks. Do not waive them because other checks pass.
Missing implementation execution remains unrun, even if its plan is sound. A working hypothesis is
not an observed audience insight, and an agent's comprehension check is not a human study.

## Verdict

Return FAIL when a hard boundary remains, otherwise a scoped editorial verdict with all UNKNOWNs
visible. A useful brief can be delivered with concerns; that does not authorize implementation or
publication of unsupported claims. Give line-specific repair instructions. After the configured
revision limit, preserve unresolved failures instead of increasing scores to force acceptance.
