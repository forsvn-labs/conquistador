# Section worksheet

Choose sections by the visitor questions they answer, not a required page formula. Every section
needs finished copy, its evidence boundary, and its relationship to the next decision. Use
[the LP review method](conversion-principles.md); do not import historical CP scores.

| Section purpose | Information to specify | Common gap to catch |
|---|---|---|
| Introduce the offer | Actual product or service, intended task, relevant limitation, next action | A promise without a clear offer |
| Explain a use | Capability, observable use, prerequisites, limits | An invented business outcome |
| Show evidence | Source, scope, permission, date, accurate representation | A placeholder presented as a customer |
| Answer a concern | Supplied concern or labeled assumption, supported answer, remaining limit | A made-up objection used to praise the product |
| Explain terms | Price, currency, period, scope, exclusions, known conditions | A material charge hidden until after commitment |
| Collect information | Field purpose, optionality, handling, validation, result, recovery | Unnecessary collection or false success state |
| Support an action | Label, destination, commitment, relevant reassurance | A request disguised as immediate access |
| Provide reference answers | Real or explicitly anticipated questions and bounded answers | Invented answers presented as support history |

## Per-section output

Write the visitor question, complete headline/body, claim sources or assumptions, and action if one
is needed. Add layout/order, responsive behavior, asset slot/fallback, and implementation states
at the requested depth. List applicable LP checks with PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN/N/A and location.
An asset is optional when text answers the question. A missing proof element should be removed from
public copy, not filled with a synthetic customer.

One final headline is sufficient. Alternatives should compare actual message choices, not fulfill
a count. A form has as many justified fields as its task requires, with unnecessary collection
removed. Prices and conditions belong where they inform the decision; do not bury them to fit a
section taxonomy. Neither the page nor a section needs a compulsory testimonial, timer, or closing block.

Inline delivery stays bounded to the requested argument and build notes. File mode uses
[format conventions](format-conventions.md). Planned implementation checks remain unrun until
executed on the selected target. Review of the brief grants no publication or deployment authority.
