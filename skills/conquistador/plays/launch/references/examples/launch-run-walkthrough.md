# Example — bounded Product Hunt and LinkedIn preparation

Facts: a founder has a working local Mac demo, no reviews or metrics, no fixed date, and no posting
credentials.

1. `campaign` chooses Product Hunt as the launch surface and LinkedIn as founder-led support,
   then marks destination, date, gallery capture, current platform rules, owners, and measurement as
   readiness cells.
2. `social` produces the Product Hunt tagline, description, maker comment, LinkedIn post, and
   response bank from the same audience, moment, mechanism, and proof boundary.
3. `copy` supplies any non-social announcement or destination blocks actually requested.
4. `creative` specifies a real demo gallery sequence. Without attached UI, it writes labeled
   capture requirements and never invents screens.
5. `outreach` is optional and runs only for named, relevant recipients with known affiliation
   and contact context.
6. The package stops before publishing because the date, current rule check, gallery, destination,
   credentials, and exact payload approval are incomplete.
7. `measure` defines a dated observation record for qualified replies, demo requests,
   activation, objections, confounders, and a keep/revise/stop decision. It does not claim results.

The delivered artifact is still useful: it contains ready-to-use copy, exact asset requirements,
reply guidance, blockers, owners, a decision signal, and one human-owned next action. No runner,
private artifact tree, or uninstalled outcome is necessary.
