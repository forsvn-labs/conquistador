# Launch chain spec

Use this composition for a focused launch package. It is a prose workflow, not a runner, publishing
tool, or hidden state machine. Every named outcome is present in the installed Conquistador package.

## Chain

1. **Position** — use `position` only when the audience, costly moment, promise,
   mechanism, or proof remains unresolved.
2. **Plan** — use `campaign` for the launch bet, named channel roles, readiness, sequence,
   asset inventory, owners, contingencies, budget boundary, and decision signal.
3. **Create** — use `social` for channel-native posts and listings, `copy` for page or
   announcement blocks, and `creative` for truthful gallery, image, or video requirements.
4. **Reach** — use `outreach` only when named recipients or a bounded supporter list is part of
   the request. Never invent recipients, enrichment, affiliation, or send authority.
5. **Coordinate** — use `event` only when timed people, rooms, live demos, or other
   operational dependencies make minute-level coordination useful.
6. **Release boundary** — assemble the ready-to-use package and stop. Publishing, credentials,
   account changes, spend, and external writes require explicit approval for the exact payload.
7. **Learn** — use `measure` to define the observation record and later make a
   keep/revise/stop decision from observed results.

Skip inapplicable steps. A small launch should not acquire ceremony merely to fill the chain.

## Pre-launch cadence

Use when the launch is weeks away and the team ships user-visible increments before it.

- Plan one public post for each shipped, user-visible increment, with the launch as the larger
  announcement at the end. Each post shows real product behavior.
- When more than one team member posts, give each person a different angle and audience. Record
  which angle draws qualified responses, and use the best-supported angle in the launch copy.
- A few days before launch, prepare a reminder for contacts who opted in. Give the date and what
  becomes available. Never ask for votes or coordinated engagement.

## Bundle contract

Return one coherent package with:

- one audience, moment, promise, proof boundary, objection, and action;
- finished native copy for every requested channel;
- asset requirements grounded in real product behavior or clearly labeled capture requirements;
- readiness blockers and owners;
- truthful response guidance and disclosure rules;
- the human release boundary;
- a dated decision signal and the evidence needed for the later readout.

For a named platform, load its installed channel note and verify volatile rules when browsing is
allowed. If current verification is unavailable, mark the rule unknown rather than claiming current
compliance. No package-internal file store, sibling repository, runtime, or unpublished skill is
required.

## Gates

- Unsupported proof, fake engagement, vote asks, invented customers, and fabricated UI are blocks.
- Missing credentials or a fixed date block publication, not preparation of the package.
- A critic result is an internal quality gate, never human approval.
- Do not imply that the launch happened or that later metrics exist.
