# Creative-asset-review workflow

Use privately to review an actual rendered image, graphic, carousel frame, share card, or similar
asset.

1. Use `brief-creative` to recover the approved communication job and acceptance criteria.
2. Use `improve-conversion` to diagnose the real rendered asset—not its prompt—across brief fidelity,
   message hierarchy, brand, legibility, accessibility, and render defects.
3. Use the `artifact-hygiene` method to verify the selected variant, file identity, dimensions,
   provenance, and regeneration boundary.

Load recovered asset-eval method under `conquistador/references/creative-asset-review/` (metric
ingest, diagnosis, recommendation, critic) instead of paraphrasing it.

Review one selected variant at a time and require the actual asset plus source brief. Separate strategic
brief failure from execution/render failure. Preserve what works; never infer deletion or cleanup
authority from a review verdict.

End with one terminal Review Packet for final human review: actual-output verdict, blocking/material
findings, what survives, exact re-render instructions, and one next action.
