# Launch-package anti-patterns

- **Ceremony for its own sake:** forcing every optional outcome into a small launch. Use only the
  steps needed for the requested channels and real dependencies.
- **One generic asset everywhere:** renaming or cropping the same copy without adapting the hook,
  format, evidence, action, or response behavior.
- **Unsupported launch proof:** invented customers, rankings, testimonials, screenshots, usage, or
  results. Missing proof becomes a boundary or capture requirement.
- **Fake support:** vote asks, coordinated fake engagement, undisclosed affiliation, or recipient
  invention.
- **Rule claims from memory:** claiming current platform compliance without checking current primary
  guidance when the rule affects the package. Mark it unverified when checking is unavailable.
- **Hidden publication:** treating finished copy or an internal critic PASS as authority to post,
  send, spend, authenticate, or change an account.
- **Open learning loop:** no observation window, qualified signal, confounder record, or
  keep/revise/stop rule.
- **Private-path dependency:** requiring a project store, runner, unpublished leaf, or sibling
  repository to prepare the package.
