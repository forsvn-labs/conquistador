# Shared evidence standard

Load when a recommendation depends on market facts, platform rules, product behavior, metrics,
benchmarks, customer language, or another claim that could be wrong or stale.

- Separate observed evidence, reasonable inference, assumption, and unknown.
- Prefer current primary sources for volatile facts and record source plus date near the claim.
- Preserve denominators, units, time windows, segments, attribution boundaries, and missing-versus-zero.
- Trace quotes and customer language to authorized sources; never fabricate or smooth them into a
  stronger claim.
- State competing explanations and what evidence would reverse a consequential conclusion.
- Use an honest dated recheck trigger instead of allowing an unknown to become a fact through age.

Evidence supports the recommendation; it never grants publish, spend, send, release, or deletion
authority.
