# Motion background format

Use when motion supports hierarchy, atmosphere, or product proof behind a primary message.

- Give motion one job and keep the main claim readable without it.
- Use real or clearly representative product material; never imply unshipped behavior.
- Define loop, duration, crop, contrast, safe zones, reduced-motion alternative, compression, and
  performance ceiling.
- Avoid decorative motion that competes with copy, causes nausea, or hides a weak argument.
- Inspect the final render on target dimensions and connection constraints.

Deliver the motion concept, source/provenance, production specification, accessibility fallback, and
actual-output acceptance.
