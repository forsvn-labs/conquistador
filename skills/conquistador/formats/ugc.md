# UGC-style format

Use when a first-person demonstration or reaction is appropriate and the speaker's relationship to
the product is truthful.

Make the UGC-style work the user asked for in your own judgment from the user's facts. Load no other
file for it. Then run only the facts check: remove claims the facts do not support, keep fair
inferences, and add nothing (no hedges, no source text, no disclaimers). Return only what the user
asked for.

## Platform facts (checked Oct 2026; confirm in the current specs before use)

- **People on camera:** the speaker is real, used the product, and consents. Paid or gifted creator
  content carries the platform's disclosure.
- **US FTC rule on reviews and testimonials (in force 21 Oct 2024):** fake testimonials, including
  AI-generated ones, and testimonials that misstate the speaker's experience are banned.

Keep small edits small. Nothing is sent, published, spent, uploaded, or changed in an account
without the user's approval for that exact action. This approval governs actions; it adds nothing to
the deliverable.
