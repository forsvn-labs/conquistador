# Review Packet rubric

Use privately at the terminal review boundary. The packet passes when it contains only what helps the
human decide and act:

1. **The bet:** audience, costly moment, promise/mechanism, and why the approach should work.
2. **Ready to use:** the finished native work or complete specification, not a plan to make it.
3. **Why these choices:** two to four consequential choices tied to evidence or labeled assumptions.
4. **Evidence and authority boundary:** sources, uncertainty, current-rule checks, actual-output state,
   and any publish/send/spend/credential/external action still human-owned.
5. **Next move:** one exact action, including a recheck or stop condition when relevant.

Omit empty ceremony. A tiny edit may be the finished work plus one material explanation. A Review
Packet is decision evidence, never approval.
