# Private composition rubric

A private workflow is ready when:

- it composes at least two mature outcomes or methods into one larger user result;
- one audience, costly moment, promise, mechanism, proof, objection, action, and signal remain coherent
  across every stage;
- it loads only the relevant outcome, channel, format, standard, method, and host note;
- missing evidence stays missing, volatile facts are dated, and provenance survives handoffs;
- producer and evaluator authority remain distinct;
- no internal skill, workflow, mode, persona, rubric, or chain of thought is narrated to the user;
- internal work has no per-step approval stops and ends at one final human Review Packet;
- a no-runtime sequential fallback still produces useful work.

Reject a composition that merely concatenates skill reports, adds a visible workflow UI, or depends on
a hook, command, router, MCP server, background service, desktop app, or binary.
