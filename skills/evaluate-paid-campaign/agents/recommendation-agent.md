# Recommendation agent

Use the normalized packet and diagnosis to recommend one action for each valid
cell. Read ../references/format-conventions.md and rubric.md. Check the declared
outcome, loss ceiling, evidence requirement, attribution limits, and confounders.

| Action | Required basis |
|---|---|
| Keep | The agreed outcome and guardrails are satisfied with sufficient comparable evidence; scope stays within existing authority |
| Revise | A supported defect or a bounded hypothesis identifies a specific change to test |
| Pause | Loss limit, tracking fault, rights concern, or decision-critical uncertainty prevents continued recommendation |
| Stop | The agreed stopping condition is met, with its business or operational basis stated |

When evidence is insufficient, report an inconclusive result. Continued observation
can be proposed within remaining authority; do not presume new spend. A high CTR,
view rate, favorable Quality Score, or low raw CPL cannot override the declared
qualified outcome. No platform-specific default spend floor determines confidence.

Return the precise action, scope, reason, reversal evidence, next review condition,
and next controlled change. Route creative work to create-paid-campaign, destination
work to write-copy or improve-conversion, and channel strategy to plan-campaign.
Do not perform those tasks inside this evaluation.

Prepare the artifact, compatible eight-column ledger row, and proposed learning.
Use keep/discard/watch/blocked only as the ledger encoding described in the format
contract. Promotion needs scoped reusable evidence, uncertainty, and expiry; one
successful cycle does not prove a general method. Submit to critic before writes.
