# Decide from a scoped evidence record

## Establish the question before interpreting results

Read the source campaign hypothesis and agreed outcome, loss ceiling, review date,
and evidence requirement. Accept operator-supplied exports, screenshots, or pasted
numbers with provenance. An existing loop is optional. Without actual delivery or
outcome evidence, return a measurement plan and blocked evaluation.

Define one cell by network, eligibility, creative, offer, destination, and period.
Separate cold acquisition from retargeting and preserve network-specific framing
such as intent_tier or targeting_mode. If a blended export cannot be separated,
report the aggregate with its limitations and block cell-specific recommendations.

## Normalize the comparison

Record counts, units, denominators, currency, dates, timezone, event definition,
attribution window, source, lag, and missing values. Compute rates from compatible
counts; never average rates without their weights. Zero outcomes means CPA is
undefined, not zero. Missing outcomes remain missing, not zero observed outcomes.

Compare the same event definition and cohort maturity. Record changes in offer,
creative, targeting, placement, spend, tracking, and qualification. Historical
comparison can support a description without proving that creative caused a change.

## Assess whether the evidence can support this decision

Use the test's declared information requirement and acceptable loss. Show how the
verdict changes under plausible late conversions, unmatched events, or small count
changes. Name the uncertainty method and assumptions for statistical claims.
There is no default dollar floor, impression count, frequency limit, or duration
that creates confidence. If the requirement was never defined, describe the result
and propose one for the next test; do not retroactively select a winning threshold.

A loss limit can justify a pause recommendation even when performance is uncertain.
Do not call that statistical proof of failure. An inconclusive test does not
justify additional spending beyond the existing authority.

## Diagnose without turning correlations into rules

For each finding, write observation, plausible cause, competing explanation, and
what evidence would distinguish them. On Meta, inspect reach, frequency, CTR,
conversion cost, negative feedback if available, and audience overlap. Rising
frequency with falling CTR is a fatigue hypothesis. Auction changes, placement
mix, lag, or tracking faults may explain the same pattern.

Read the matching Google Ads, TikTok Ads, or LinkedIn Ads metric pack when relevant.
No diagnostic score or viewing rate alone predicts a business outcome. Retargeting
attribution does not establish incremental conversions.

## Decide and retain a reversible record

Recommend keep, revise, pause, or stop with scope, evidence, reversal condition,
and one controlled next test where useful. Use format-conventions.md to translate
to the existing keep/discard/watch/blocked ledger without losing the precise action.
Keep acceptance separate from permission to change live state.

Write the evaluation before appending its ledger row. Promote a learning only when
its evidence, uncertainty, scope, and expiry support reuse. One creative refresh
that coincides with improvement does not establish a universal fatigue threshold.
