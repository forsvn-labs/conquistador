# Motion background: decision and test method

This pack specifies work to perform. It contains no platform performance benchmarks or verified ranking formula. A method edit is not a platform verification. Use supplied account evidence and dated primary platform requirements for the actual task. When verification is unavailable, record the missing constraint and hand off the check.

## 0. When not to use this channel or format

Use motion only when it explains a process or supports the intended visual hierarchy. Prefer a still image when movement competes with text, adds loading cost without useful information or cannot respect the viewer's motion preference.

## 1. Hook and message choices

Choose an opening by the evidence it can deliver. These choices have no performance tier or prescribed timing window.

| Choice | Opening decision | Required payoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Process cue | Show a restrained change related to the product task. | Keep the cue subordinate to the foreground message. |
| State transition | Represent a meaningful before and after state. | Make both states understandable in a still fallback. |
| Spatial context | Show where a foreground element belongs. | Avoid movement that the viewer must track to read the page. |

Record the audience, desired decision, supported claim and visible evidence before drafting. Reject an opening whose promised answer the asset does not provide.

## 2. Format constraints and production handoff

Specify container dimensions, crop behavior, asset budget, poster, loop seam, contrast and reduced-motion fallback. Test keyboard access and pointer behavior for the foreground controls. Confirm asset licenses and avoid unnecessary audio.

The handoff must identify the destination account, format, verified constraints and their dates, asset paths, preview checks, accessibility work, rights status and any unresolved upload requirement. Keep production settings separate from claims about what the platform rewards.

## 3. Signals to measure

Measure loading cost, text readability, task completion and motion-related review findings. Decorative loop counts are not a business outcome. Record device and network conditions for any performance observation.

For each selected measure, record its definition, numerator, denominator, observation period, export source and missing data. Choose a business outcome and a diagnostic measure before inspecting results. If no account baseline exists, collect one; do not replace it with a claimed industry average.

## 4. Anti-patterns

Do not embed the only product explanation or CTA inside decorative video. Do not ignore reduced motion, intercept input or claim animation improves conversion without a suitable test.

Do not call a draft published, a preview a platform receipt, or a method review a live test. Missing evidence remains unknown.

## 5. Playbook and test decision

1. Record the user outcome, audience, destination and available evidence. Apply the fit decision in section 0.
2. Select one opening from section 1, or describe another opening with an equally explicit payoff. Draft the complete asset and next action.
3. Resolve the format and rights checks in section 2. Record the preview result and owner of any unresolved check.
4. Write the comparison below as a test plan. Name the changed variable, comparison, observation window, resource cap and stop condition.
5. Deliver the asset, production instructions and review packet. Publication or sending requires separate authorization for the exact action.
6. If authorized results later arrive, compare them using the recorded metric definitions. Return keep, revise, stop or insufficient evidence, with the reason and next test.

Compare the page with a still poster and with motion using the same foreground content. Inspect contrast at every scene state and test the reduced-motion setting. Reject motion if it hides information or prevents the primary task.

## 6. Timing and capacity

Set duration and cadence from the content needed, production capacity, audience context and response coverage. Set the measurement window from the decision latency and available sample. Do not prescribe a posting frequency, time-of-day advantage or retention cutoff without task evidence. Stop a test at its approved resource cap even if the result is inconclusive.

## 7. CTA and conversion path

Keep the action in accessible foreground content. Verify it remains visible, clickable and keyboard reachable while the background loads, plays, pauses or fails.

Record every step between the asset and the desired outcome. Distinguish attempted actions from completed actions. Hand off the exact destination, tracking parameters if used, access terms and response owner with the final copy.

## 8. Open questions and decision record

List unresolved account capabilities, audience fit, asset rights, exposure comparability and attribution gaps. For each gap, state whether it blocks production, blocks publication or only limits interpretation. Carry the result with its date and scope; a successful local test does not become a universal platform rule.
