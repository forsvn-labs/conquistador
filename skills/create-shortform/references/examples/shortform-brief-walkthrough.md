# Short-form brief walkthrough

This newly authored hypothetical walkthrough demonstrates decisions and handoffs. No production, platform verification or performance test was executed.

## Request and available evidence

A user needs a short video that explains how to move a draft to its next reviewer. The user supplies a permitted demonstration account and product instructions. For this example, these are scenario assumptions, not evidence collected during repository work. The selected destinations are TikTok and Instagram Reels.

## Resolve the brief

The format agent records the destinations, account requirements and pending verification. It does not import fixed duration or caption thresholds. The evidence owner identifies the available product instruction and whether the proposed claim needs additional support. The production-mode agent chooses screen capture with voice-over and records access, caption and poster requirements.

## Opening choice

The recommended opening shows the current reviewer and the next required action. One alternative starts with the completed handoff; another asks who is responsible when the first reviewer cannot decide. These are different entry choices into the same supported task. No archetype popularity is claimed.

The reviewer checks that each opening's answer appears in the later demonstration. A version claiming automatic approval is rejected because the instructions require a person. The revision says who decides and shows the handoff rather than implying automation.

## Production plan

| Sequence | Required visible action | Production detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Identify the current state and decision owner. | Use readable account-cleared screen capture and exact spoken text. |
| Demonstration | Select the next reviewer and submit the handoff. | Keep each decisive step visible; set timings from a read-through. |
| Result | Show the new state and any remaining approval. | Preserve the condition in text and speech. |
| Next action | Offer the setup guide. | Verify its destination and account link path before publication. |

The storyboard carries exact time ranges, framing, overlay text and audio cues before production. The audio plan uses original voice-over with pronunciation and caption guidance. Any music proposal needs a rights record and cue map.

## Destination variants

The variant agent previews the primary version in the Reels context. It records retained and changed choices for the opening, crop, audio, caption, duration and CTA. A crop change may be useful, but only after the full destination assessment. There is no required music switch or retention-based timing formula.

## Critic review

- Hook checks task clarity and the promised answer.
- Production checks executable timing, assets, readable evidence and audio direction.
- The compatible `algorithm-fit` check reviews actual format, accessibility and measurement-plan fit.
- Brand-fit checks the supplied voice, truthful product role and claim support.

Each failure routes to its owner. After at most two rewrite cycles, a standing failure remains an operator concern. Language polish follows substantive acceptance and cannot authorize a blocked action.

## Delivery and later learning

Deliver the complete primary and variant briefs, claim/source record, pending format and rights checks, exact text, production manifest and proposed test. Use `pack_verified: none` until an applicable platform check occurs. Identify the method revision separately.

A later authorized test can compare the task-first and result-first openings using comparable account data. It must define the outcome, denominator, baseline, window, resource cap and interpretation limits before execution. This walkthrough contains no observed result or assertion that either opening wins.
