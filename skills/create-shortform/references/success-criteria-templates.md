# Short-form success and measurement plan

Define success for the user's outcome before production. Separate an artifact acceptance check from a performance target. A readable, accurate video can pass production review while its commercial effect remains unknown.

## Required measurement record

| Field | Required decision |
| --- | --- |
| Outcome | Name what the viewer should understand or complete. |
| Primary measure | Define the action, numerator, denominator and reporting source. |
| Diagnostic measure | Name the viewing or response measure that can locate a problem. |
| Baseline | Identify a comparable account cohort and its period, or state none available. |
| Target | Use an operator-approved objective or a justified comparison to the baseline; show the basis. |
| Observation window | Account for the action's delay and reporting latency. |
| Resources and stop | Set the production/spend cap and conditions for pausing. |
| Interpretation limits | Record exposure differences, missing data, paid support and changed definitions. |

When no baseline or measurement access exists, propose baseline collection and an operator handoff. Do not invent an industry average or import a distribution threshold from a research pack.

## Choose diagnostic observations

| Platform or format | Useful question | Required caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok or Reels | Where does the viewer leave the demonstration, and what is on screen then? | Confirm the export's view and retention definitions; a drop is not proof of an algorithm penalty. |
| Shorts | Is the answer complete, and do interested viewers find the related resource? | Distinguish counted views, engaged views and onward actions if available. |
| YouTube long-form | Does the opening fulfill the title/thumbnail promise? | Segment traffic sources before comparing clicks or retention. |
| LinkedIn or X | Do relevant readers understand and act on the task described? | Separate relevant questions, visits and completed actions from raw replies. |
| Cross-platform variants | Does the action path work in each destination? | Do not compare different denominators or audiences as a controlled format test. |

## Diagnose without claiming causation

1. Compare the published artifact to the approved brief. Record concrete execution differences with timestamps.
2. Verify the measurement definition, export period and data completeness.
3. Locate the observed change in the viewing or action path. Inspect the corresponding content.
4. List plausible explanations, including exposure differences, offer fit and tracking failure.
5. Choose one test that separates those explanations. State what result would weaken the proposed explanation.

Low reach alone cannot diagnose audio, length or an opening failure. High completion does not establish product fit. Lack of a measured action may reflect missing tracking; say so before recommending a creative rewrite.

## Brief template

```markdown
## Success Criteria

- Outcome: [specific task or decision]
- Artifact acceptance: [claim support, readable demonstration, accessible text, working next action]
- Primary measure: [definition, source and denominator]
- Baseline and target basis: [account evidence or no baseline]
- Diagnostic measure: [definition and why it helps]
- Observation window: [period and reporting delay]
- Resource cap and stop condition: [approved scope]
- Next comparison: [one changed variable and what would contradict the hypothesis]
- Missing evidence: [owner and next check]
```

A completed draft does not satisfy a live performance target. Keep planned tests, observed results and operator approval distinct.
