# Short-form failure checks

Use these checks while drafting and during the four-part critic review. Name the exact artifact location and route each failure to its owner. Do not reject work merely because it uses an opening absent from a catalog.

| Failure | How to inspect it | Correction owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening promise lacks support | Compare the opening claim with the demonstrated result and evidence. | Hook agent and evidence owner. |
| Placeholder production instruction | Find actions such as show product without visible details, timing or framing. | Storyboard agent. |
| Text, image and speech conflict | Compare the meaning of each at the same shot and across the payoff. | Hook and storyboard agents. |
| Important information is inaccessible | Preview sound-off, captions, crop, overlays and readable duration. | Format and storyboard agents. |
| Unspecified or unauthorized audio | Inspect track identity, rights and cue map, or voice-over recording direction. | Audio agent. |
| Missing production needs | Check talent, equipment, location, source assets and permission per shot. Mixed mode also needs its transition plan. | Production-mode agent. |
| Borrowed metric used as a rule | Trace each target and any claim about platform preference to applicable evidence. | Format agent and measurement owner. |
| Unchecked next action | Follow the stated route and verify access, destination and terms. | Caption/CTA agent. |
| Fabricated customer language | Compare each claimed quote with its authorized source. | Voice-of-customer agent. |
| Unsupported testimonial or product result | Inspect the actual result, speaker relationship and permitted use. | Evidence owner. |
| Variant has no destination assessment | Inspect the What Changed From Hero table and complete variant brief. Retained choices need reasons too. | Platform variant agent. |
| Product task disappears under promotion | Find where the asset demonstrates the promised useful task. Remove or move interruptions that obscure it. | Hook and storyboard agents. |

## Evidence limitations

Missing audience context, incomplete account verification, unequal exposure and a small sample must remain explicit. They can limit a draft or evaluation without proving creative failure. Unresolved rights, false claims and unavailable destinations block the affected action. Never use a date-only freshness label to certify unsupported platform mechanics.

## Contract and workflow checks

Preserve the primary and variant artifact identity, exact text, shot timing and source links. The producer must not guess how to combine assets. If a field or body section changes, update the corresponding consumers rather than leaving conflicting instructions.

Use the existing market/brand language-polish route. A polish pass cannot clear a substantive critic failure. Stop after the allowed rewrite cycles and present unresolved concerns for the operator. The internal `done_with_concerns` grade is not publish, send or spend authority.

Do not silently drop a requested platform or expand the authorized production scope. When the request needs more work than the declared capacity, expose the missing resource and deliver the complete bounded portion with its limits.
