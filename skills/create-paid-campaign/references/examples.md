# Paid creative comparison examples

These are explicitly invented instructional scenarios. They are not customer
proof, benchmark measurements, live calls, or release evidence.

## A capability claim with no customer statistics

The fictional product Slotnote converts a supplied meeting agenda into assigned
action items. Its example brief permits only that capability claim. The offer is
a product tour at https://example.invalid/tour, with no required signup.

Hero: "Turn your meeting agenda into assigned action items. See Slotnote work."
Variant A: "Who owns the next step? Turn your agenda into assigned action items
with Slotnote. See it work."
Variant B: "Leave the meeting with assigned action items. See Slotnote turn an
agenda into the next steps."

The opening framing changes; the capability, offer, destination, and visual remain
controls. Each actual platform component still needs current format verification.
No customer count or research citation is added merely to make the ad specific.

## An unsupported claim

Rejected: "Slotnote doubles team productivity in a week. Thousands trust it."
The brief supplies neither productivity measurements nor a customer count. Remove
both claims. A disclaimer would not make them supported.

## A proxy event

A fictional app test can observe trial starts before paid renewals. The proposed
measurement plan labels trial_start a proxy and schedules the paid-outcome review
after the specified trial and reporting delay. It does not infer an Apple window
or declare a trial-start winner profitable.

## A production choice

An authorized creator clip is cheaper to adapt than a new demonstration, but its
spoken offer is outdated. Compare correction cost, available rights, and production
time. If it cannot be corrected truthfully, use another asset. No assumed spend
ceiling decides between dedicated and repurposed-ugc.
