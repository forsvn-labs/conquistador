# Paid creation failure checks

Apply these checks to the brief and finished assets. Identify the exact passage
and its consequence; do not fail work for avoiding a borrowed framework.

| Defect | Detection | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Unsupported authority | Private notes or named practitioners justify a performance rule | Derive the decision from supplied account evidence and declare uncertainty |
| Borrowed benchmark | A fixed CTR, budget share, volume quota, frequency cap, or attribution window drives the plan | Specify an account-scoped loss and observation rule |
| Fabricated specificity | A number or source appears without permitted evidence | Remove it and describe supported product behavior |
| Confounded comparison | Copy, proof, audience, and offer change under a one-variable claim | Restore controls or label concept exploration |
| False certainty | A diagnostic metric is said to prove the cause or predict a winner | State competing explanations and a discriminating test |
| Destination mismatch | The ad promises a different price, action, or capability from the destination | Correct the ad or block pending destination correction |
| Unlicensed depiction | Creator footage, voice, or quote lacks permission | Use an authorized asset or another truthful format |
| Hidden sponsorship | A commercial or scripted scene appears to be independent testimony | Add accurate disclosure and representative-scene labeling |
| Unverified format gate | An old cap or style preference is called a current platform rule | Obtain current scoped evidence or mark unresolved |
| Format hierarchy | Dedicated creative is assumed to scale better than repurposed-ugc | Compare actual rights, cost, suitability, and measured results |
| Proxy success | Installs, trial starts, or clicks stand in for paid outcomes without validation | Retain the proxy label and schedule downstream measurement |
| Polish regression | An edit drops terms, qualifiers, proof meaning, or URL | Restore the affected variant and rerun checks |
| Approval confusion | Critic PASS is treated as permission to launch | Return the review packet for separate human action approval |

No unsupported source quotation belongs in the artifact rationale. Customer quotes
require exact wording, context, and permitted use. Lack of customer proof permits
truthful capability copy; it does not justify invented testimonial text.
