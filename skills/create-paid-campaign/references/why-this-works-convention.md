# Explain the product decision

Place a short rationale after the finished assets and before the scorecard. In the
three-file campaign contract this belongs in [slug].rationale.md.

State the test hypothesis, the product or audience evidence behind the chosen
message, the controlled components, and what outcome would challenge the choice.
Link each factual assertion to the supplied source. Explain what the capability
lets the user do; do not assert a proprietary advantage or expected win without
evidence.

Use complete statements such as: the supplied demonstration shows assignment of
an owner, so this variant tests whether naming that task improves qualified tour
completion. This is an example of reasoning structure, not a measured claim.

If evidence is partial, mark the unsupported part as a hypothesis and state what
would resolve it. If no audience research exists, use the known product and offer
facts without inventing buyer language or hidden motives. Do not claim that a
product is uniquely superior merely because its name appears in the copy.

Platform requirements and verification limits belong in legibility-convention.md.
This rationale explains the product-specific choice and causal limits. It cannot
replace the evidence ledger, critic result, human acceptance, or launch authority.
