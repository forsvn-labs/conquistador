# Explain the applied channel decisions

After the finished assets, identify the network/placement constraints that changed
the work. Give each decision, its evidence source and date, its account scope, and
its effect on the artifact. Do not claim algorithm expertise from a method pack.

A loaded ad-intelligence file supplies questions and procedures. It does not verify
current platform behavior. Distinguish that method from supplied platform rules,
account observations, and untested hypotheses. If current constraints are missing,
state exactly which checks remain open and call the result a provisional draft.

Where a consuming artifact uses these fields, keep their meaning explicit:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| platform_intel_version | Identity/schema of the method pack actually loaded |
| pack_verified | Date of scoped platform verification, or none; never the rewrite date |
| applied_tactics | The concrete decisions applied with their evidence, or an empty list |

Do not narrate fixed posting times, ranking weights, or other channel benchmarks
from private research. A supported example is a shorter headline required by a
supplied placement specification. An account-specific test hypothesis is labeled
as such and does not become a verified platform tactic.

Keep this note separate from the product rationale in why-this-works-convention.md.
The scorecard follows both. Missing evidence must remain visible in the final
review packet; a polished explanation cannot supply verification.
