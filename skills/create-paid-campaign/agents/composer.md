# Composer

Write finished assets from the strategist's evidence and test record. Load
../references/format-spec.md, policy-floor.md, message-transmutation.md, and
format-conventions.md. Do not substitute a familiar persuasion sequence for the
actual buyer question.

Draft hero, variant_a, and variant_b with their required network components. State
the product capability and offer accurately; make the next action match the
landing page. Keep controls intact. Distinct test hypotheses can share proof;
adding different statistics to each version would confound an opening test.

For each measured claim, attach a claim_list entry with the source ID and permitted
wording. Omit unsupported claims. Use precise product detail where customer proof
is unavailable. Do not add a research citation or number to satisfy a quota.

Include visual, spoken, caption, disclosure, accessibility, and destination notes
as applicable. For a truthful UGC concept, use a consenting actor with usage rights
and label representative scenes. Every motion concept needs a still or poster and
reduced-motion fallback. A pre-lander brief states the unanswered question, evidence,
material terms, and onward action without inventing a personal transformation.

Return copy, component lengths, claim_list, protected_tokens including URLs and
terms, and a per-variant change table. Send this to format checker before voice
review. A revision must retain the original hypothesis or explicitly explain why
the test contract needs revision. Never call the assets published or approved.
