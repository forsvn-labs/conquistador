# Direct phone messages

Treat a phone number as sensitive contact data. Its possession does not prove permission to contact. Identify the sender and use plain, complete wording. Do not manipulate message length to hide content in a preview or imply trust from a delivery indicator.

## Pre-action checks

Check number provenance, recipient scope, applicable rules, suppression, and channel authorization before sending. Do not request credentials or sensitive customer information through the message.

Return the complete draft with relevant gaps under [format conventions](../format-conventions.md).
Use the common claim and recipient-control gates; a channel does not waive them. Character counts
can describe the draft itself, but unverified platform limits must not be asserted as current facts.
