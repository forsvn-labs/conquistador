# Public replies and private messages

For a public reply, contribute to the actual conversation and assume the content is public. Do not expose private evidence or turn every reply into a product pitch. A private message must accurately disclose affiliation and purpose.

## Pre-action checks

Verify current platform limits and contact rules before action. Public posting is not permission to receive a private campaign. Do not invent a relationship based on a follow or like.

Return the complete draft with relevant gaps under [format conventions](../format-conventions.md).
Use the common claim and recipient-control gates; a channel does not waive them. Character counts
can describe the draft itself, but unverified platform limits must not be asserted as current facts.
