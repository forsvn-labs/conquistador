# Email

Provide an accurate subject and a complete body. Never imply an existing thread with a fake reply prefix. Make affiliation and the requested decision easy to find. Do not add tracking links or attachments without sender review.

## Pre-action checks

Before sending, the operator must verify sender identity, address selection, applicable regional requirements, suppression, unsubscribe handling, delivery configuration, and the approved batch/cadence. A draft cannot verify deliverability.

Return the complete draft with relevant gaps under [format conventions](../format-conventions.md).
Use the common claim and recipient-control gates; a channel does not waive them. Character counts
can describe the draft itself, but unverified platform limits must not be asserted as current facts.
