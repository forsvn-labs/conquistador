# Outreach review rubric

This is a local editorial rubric. Scores express review judgments, not measured effectiveness.
Score the actual message with a cited phrase and a concrete repair for every weak dimension.

| Dimension (0–10) | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| Peer voice | Clear, appropriate language; honest affiliation; no disguised commercial purpose |
| Signal connection | Observation matches its source; inference is limited; or conditional commercial relevance is explicit |
| CTA friction | One understandable decision, with material commitments disclosed and refusal possible |
| Recipient relevance | Context helps the recipient decide; no sender biography or irrelevant personal detail |
| Specificity | Claims have bounded meaning and support; research questions distinguish actual behaviors |

0–2: absent or misleading. 3–5: material repair needed. 6–7: usable with a stated limitation.
8–10: clear and well supported for this request. There is no automatic score floor for a route.
On replies, Signal connection assesses fidelity to the inbound; CTA friction assesses the next step,
including a correctly closed conversation. Do not score enthusiasm as consent.

## Readiness and scoring

For targeted or commercial READY drafts, use N/50. PASS requires every dimension at least 6 and
total at least 35. Totals 35–39 carry `done_with_concerns`; 40 or more may be `done`.
For no-pitch NEEDS_SIGNAL templates, Signal connection is N/A and the denominator is 40. PASS
requires all four scored dimensions at least 6 and total at least 24; 24–27 carries concerns,
28 or more may be a completed template. Neither score changes NEEDS_SIGNAL into targeted readiness.
Campaign callers may select only totals of at least 40/50 or 32/40; the stricter caller threshold
still does not authorize sending or promote a template into a verified targeted message.

## Failures override the score

Fabricated observations, unsupported claims, hidden sales intent, false identity, deceptive threading,
pressure based on invented scarcity, repitching after opt-out, and unauthorized external action
fail regardless of total. A generic research question may be factually safe yet fail for being too
vague to produce a useful answer. Missing evidence is not repaired by more persuasive wording.

After two revision cycles, report the actual failure and remaining gap. Never round up, waive a
hard gate, or return PASS merely because time or revision budget ended.
