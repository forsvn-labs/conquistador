# Selection evidence

Record source, date, access class (public, operator-supplied, permissioned), exact observation,
recipient match, and the limited inference used in the draft. Keep an internal source excerpt only
as long as needed to verify the statement. Do not reproduce private or sensitive details in copy.

A public product change may justify asking about that change; it does not prove workload, budget,
or dissatisfaction. A team announcement does not identify who owns a purchase. A job title and
company category explain a segment but do not establish an individual event.

If two sources disagree, mark the conflict and avoid the disputed assertion. If a source is old,
ask whether the situation is still current rather than silently updating it. If a URL cannot be
read, say it was not verified; do not treat the URL itself as evidence of its alleged content.

Targeted research needs a real signal. Missing signal yields a generic NEEDS_SIGNAL template.
Commercial drafting may use a conditional use case with its targeting uncertainty disclosed.
Neither route allows a fabricated observed-person opener or enrichment without authorization.
