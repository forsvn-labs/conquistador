# Campaign experiments

Use an experiment only when it resolves a decision the user needs to make. These procedures require task-specific evidence and resource limits. None supplies a growth forecast, external practitioner rule or preset uplift.

## Trial access duration

Use when users may need more time to complete the activation task and the product can vary access terms safely. Identify the task, time-to-value evidence, serving cost, refund/cancellation behavior and existing commitments. Compare feasible durations selected from that evidence; do not automatically double the current duration.

Before an authorized test, define allocation, eligibility, offer disclosure, primary outcome, observation horizon and guardrails. Compare paid conversion after both groups have had a fair opportunity to convert. Include support cost, retained usage and refunds. Do not assume cancellations occur on a particular day. A result measured before one trial ends is not a fair conversion comparison.

## Re-engagement message

Use when a lapsed audience has a permitted reason to receive the proposed message and the product offers something relevant. Verify the audience source, current permission, suppressions and channel rules for this exact purpose. Prior opt-in alone is not a universal clearance for every message.

Select one reason to return from actual product changes or the user's known task. Draft the needed message and any justified follow-up; do not require a fixed sequence or false last-call deadline. Plan a comparison with an untreated group where appropriate and authorized. Measure qualified reactivation and resulting value after costs, alongside unsubscribes and complaints. No message is sent by writing this plan.

## Community answer discovery

Use when supplied research identifies a relevant, active discussion and the team can contribute an accurate answer. Check the specific community rules, affiliation disclosure and current thread context. Search visibility can be evidence of discovery but does not make insertion of a product mention appropriate.

Prepare a self-contained answer that addresses the actual question. Include a destination only where relevant and allowed. Do not manufacture posts, coordinate votes, repeat promotional replies or assume that a removed contribution should be reposted. After authorized participation, record useful questions, moderation outcomes and attributable visits when available; describe untracked influence as unknown.

## Paid-search result review

Use only with actual authorized paid-search data and an agreed evaluation scope. Identify the platform, campaign, query or keyword reporting level, conversion event, attribution window and reporting delay. Reconcile spend, conversions and revenue definitions before comparing rows.

Segment by intent, match behavior, destination and cohort maturity where data permit. A competitor term or low-volume query is not automatically waste. Record which rows have enough evidence for a decision, which need a limited test and which remain unresolved. Propose one change with its budget cap, comparison and stop condition. A before/after change without control cannot establish incremental lift.

## Shared test record

Each experiment records the decision, hypothesis, changed variable, comparison, eligibility, evidence source, metric definitions, resource cap, observation window and conditions for stopping. Keep cost unknown when not measured. Report keep, revise, stop or insufficient evidence with the reasons and the next responsible owner.
