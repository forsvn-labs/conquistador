# Campaign channel decisions

Compare channels by audience access, the task the message supports, production capacity, permissions and measurable outcomes. A growth-motion label does not establish channel priority. An audience research report can support a choice; distinguish measured behavior from an assumption.

## Channel inventory

For a full plan, record select, test or skip for each category. For a bounded parent request, consider only the supplied campaign job and candidate destinations.

| Category | Candidate uses | Checks that determine fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search engines/AEO | An answer, comparison or destination found through search. | Relevant queries, inspectable product facts, destination quality and measurement access. |
| Store/Listing platforms | Product discovery or access through a marketplace. | Listing eligibility, product availability, account access and accurate terms. |
| Bounty/Info platforms | Referrals, affiliate programs or paid distribution. | Participant fit, incentive economics, disclosure, rights and fraud controls. |
| News | A factual announcement or report useful to an external audience. | News value, claim support, permissions and an accountable response owner. |
| Forums/Communities | A useful contribution to a specific group. | Exact community rules, affiliation disclosure and value without forced promotion. |
| Social media | A post, demonstration, discussion or campaign update. | Audience evidence, suitable format, production capacity and a usable next action. |
| IRL | Events, demonstrations, out-of-home or point-of-sale material. | Location, access, production logistics, accessibility, capture and follow-up authority. |
| Mailbox | Expected email, onboarding, newsletter or permitted outreach. | Audience source, purpose, suppression, sender controls and exact sending authority. |
| SMS | A permitted transactional or promotional message. | Consent scope, message format, sender identity, unsubscribe and destination. |

Use actual platform names only to identify a selected destination or integration. Do not assume that a named publication, creator or community endorses the product or offers access.

## Audience-to-channel record

For each candidate, record the persona or task, specific account/community/search context, evidence source/date, observed behavior and missing information. A person may read without replying, search before buying or act after a later conversation. Do not force every user through a fixed sequence of awareness stages.

Choose the channel's role by the action you need to support. An explanation can build understanding; a comparison can support a decision; a working link can enable a trial. Verify that this audience can use that action in this destination. No opening type or emotional label automatically maps to a platform.

## Cost and capacity

Estimate creation, review, permissions, distribution and response effort separately. Record quoted paid costs, uncertain costs and the approved cap. Organic publication still requires work. Check dependencies and response coverage before choosing cadence. Do not assign fixed days-to-impact or proportional scaling to a channel category.

## Source and derivative assets

Select a source because it answers a useful question with authorized evidence. For each proposed derivative, record the claim, source section or timecode, context required, target format, rights, production cost and action path. Produce only derivatives that have a distinct use.

Review each derivative independently. It must not change the source's meaning or add unsupported results. Link back when useful and permitted; no universal backlink rule overrides community norms or the user's task. Schedule by readiness, audience context and response capacity. Use observed fatigue and useful outcomes to decide whether to repeat, revise or retire an asset; no fixed derivative quota or calendar expiry is supplied.

For recorded material, load `distribution-models/clipping-and-live.md` and preserve excerpt context and participant permissions.

## Referral and product-sharing tests

A referral proposal requires existing user value, a reason the recipient would benefit, clear consent and incentive terms, a qualifying event, exclusions, abuse detection, reward cost and a delivery owner. Compare reward structures using actual economics and the user's objective. There is no mandatory tier count, invite quota, cash ban or waitlist-first rule.

A product-sharing proposal identifies the user-owned artifact, who can see it, what branding or attribution appears, how the user controls sharing and what happens when access is revoked. Never expose private content or add marketing to a user's output without the permitted scope. Do not assume a particular pricing plan must remove branding.

A useful public reference needs accurate content, maintainable facts, an accessible destination and a clear owner. It may earn voluntary distribution, but a guide or original dataset does not guarantee links, ranking or acquisition. Record update triggers and any restrictions on reuse.

## Assignment and handoff

Give each selected channel a specific angle, format, owner, audience basis, next-action route, measure definition, observation window and stop rule. Identify overlapping audiences and attribution limits. Use `platform-channels.md` for supported platform packs and `growth-play-patterns.md` for bounded experiments.

Return the selected channels, alternatives considered, skipped channels with reasons, resource totals and pending checks. Publication, contact, account changes and spending require separate authorization. A campaign plan is a proposal, not a receipt of execution.
