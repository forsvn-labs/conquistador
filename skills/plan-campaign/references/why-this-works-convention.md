# Explain the campaign decision

Place one Why This Works section after the finished plan and before Review Gate.
For a bounded parent request, include it only if useful to the requested deliverable.

State the campaign hypothesis, the reader decision it serves, the evidence behind
the selected angles, and the material tradeoffs in scope or capacity. Name what
observation would challenge the plan. Cite actual product, audience, or supplied
account evidence; label assumptions and missing inputs.

A rationale can explain a useful capability without claiming that only this brand
can provide it. Do not require every sentence to imply unique superiority, hidden
motivation, or a predicted win. Per-channel verification details belong in the
channel owner's legibility-convention.md, not in a fabricated product-fit claim.

Keep the artifact first and review last. A persuasive rationale does not establish
measured results, supply missing proof, or grant human action authority.
