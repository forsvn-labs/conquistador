# Format an allocation for review

Return the proposal inline or in the requested artifact location. Include skill, date, draft status, objective, budget, currency, period, and mode.

Use an allocation table with channel or experiment, base amount, range, purpose, evidence, floor and source, owner, and timing. Keep the reserve as an explicit row and show the reconciliation formula.

Separate observed outcomes from scenario assumptions. Record missing metrics as unknown. Include alternatives, downside/base/upside cases, capacity and unit-economics checks, concentration constraints, stop rules, and review triggers.

For a proposed reallocation, state the source amount, destination, condition, and approval needed. Do not describe it as completed. Finish with the recommendation and the unresolved inputs most likely to change it.

The artifact must be usable without private research notes or a named practitioner's framework.
