### Conquistador vision

Conquistador gives marketers, founders, growth teams, and product builders
reusable marketing methods inside their existing coding or deployed agent.
One skill routes a requested outcome to a command or a play and produces one
complete, reviewable deliverable. The host supplies the model, context, tools,
and permissions.

#### Outcomes and scope

Users can create and revise positioning, launch plans, content, outreach,
ads, growth experiments, and product specifications without rebuilding the
process for every task. Skills, CLI, MCP, and native plugins expose the same
playbooks. Server applications can consume the same library.

Success means a useful result on a real task, a focused correction, and
verified use of the appropriate playbooks. Installation, a deterministic
check, and a fixture pass establish narrower facts.

#### Boundaries

Conquistador starts no background job, autonomous campaign, or provider
write through installation. Sends, publication, spend, feedback disclosure,
and reusable memory retain the applicable human decisions. Credentials and
private customer or workspace material stay outside public source.

The [product behavior contract](docs/PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md) defines detailed
installation, method, host, and review requirements. [STATUS.md](STATUS.md)
distinguishes delivered behavior from remaining acceptance.
