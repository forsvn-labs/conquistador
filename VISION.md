# Vision

Owner: Hung · Confirmed: 2026-11-10 (from Hung's own words; shipping approved the same day)

Money flows where attention goes. We build Conquistador so that every marketer,
founder, growth team, and product builder can do growth work the way the best
practitioners do it: start from the funnel, aim at people who pay, win their
attention, and learn from every result. It lives inside the agent they already use.

## Why we exist

Most marketing work is done in pieces. A post here, a campaign there, a landing page
that nobody connects to the number that matters. Teams rebuild the same process for
every task and still cannot say which work moved a customer closer to paying. Agents
make this faster, not better: they produce more content with no idea which funnel it
serves. Now that every team works with an agent, the agent should carry the
practitioner's discipline, not only the practitioner's output.

## The world we are building

A founder opens their agent and, before it writes a word, it knows how a stranger
becomes their paying customer. Every plan, page, post, and outreach sequence names
the stage it moves and the number that will show it. When growth stalls, the agent
diagnoses the cause from evidence instead of guessing, scores the ideas, and runs the
next test. Small teams compete with large ones because their agent thinks like a
growth lead. Conquistador is known as the method behind that work, used across every
agent and surface people already have.

## Who we build for

Marketers, founders, growth teams, sales and GTM teams, and product builders who use
coding or deployed agents and are judged by business results: users, revenue, or time
saved. Server applications can consume the same library.

We do not build for people who want volume without a target, or for autonomous
campaigns that run without a human deciding.

## What we believe

- The funnel is the most important thing. Identify the right funnel for the product
  first: attention, conversion, in-product engagement, and, where the industry buys
  through people, sales. Everything we make contributes to it.
- Money flows where attention goes. Solve a big problem in a small way, not a small
  problem in a big way, for people who already pay and already search for a fix.
- Know who pays, for which problem, in which ecosystem, and what they need inside.
  Their core desires (the seven deadly sins) decide the offer.
- Upper-funnel results are 40% audience, 40% offer, and 20% copy. Meet each buyer at
  their stage of awareness, with one integrated message across channels.
- Diagnose before you prescribe: root causes, hypotheses, the data that proves them,
  then action. Grow in a loop: ideas, ICE scores, targets, tests, and attribution.
- One finished, reviewable deliverable beats a pile of drafts. The human decides every
  send, publication, spend, and external write.

## How we get there

- Now: make the funnel the first thing Conquistador records and the spine of every
  growth task. Prove it on real tasks: deliverables that name their stage and metric,
  judged useful by practitioners in blind comparisons.
- Next: make diagnosis, prioritization, campaigns, and measurement follow the same
  spine, so a result in one feeds the next.
- Later: Conquistador is the growth operating method across every agent surface,
  with each team's own playbooks competing fairly beside ours.

## What we will never become

- A content machine that produces volume with no funnel stage or target.
- An autonomous campaign runner that sends, posts, or spends without a human decision.
- A source of invented numbers, quotes, customers, or results.
- A home for credentials or private customer material in public source.

## How it sustains itself

The same method library serves skills, the CLI, MCP, and native plugins. The host
supplies the model, context, tools, and permissions; Conquistador supplies the method.

## Bets

- A short doctrine shared by every command will improve results more than longer
  per-command procedures. Source: drafted 2026-11-10. Test: blind comparisons on real tasks.
- Additions accepted for doctrine 1.0 when Hung approved shipping on 2026-11-10, still
  unproven: fix the stage with the largest realistic gain first, price shapes the sales
  motion, fear often decides business purchases, judge a channel with two kinds of
  evidence when feasible, and look for loops on top of funnels.
