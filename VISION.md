# Vision

Owner: Hung · Confirmed: 2026-10-23 (accepted in PR #85; restructured 2026-11-01)

Conquistador gives marketers, founders, growth teams, and product builders
reusable marketing methods inside their existing coding or deployed agent.

## Why it exists

Users can create and revise positioning, launch plans, content, outreach,
ads, growth experiments, and product specifications without rebuilding the
process for every task.

## Who it is for

Marketers, founders, growth teams, and product builders using coding or
deployed agents. Server applications can consume the same library.

## The outcome that matters most

One complete, reviewable deliverable from a requested outcome, routed by one
skill to a command or a play.

## What done looks like

A useful result on a real task, a focused correction, and verified use of the
appropriate playbooks. Installation, a deterministic check, and a fixture
pass establish narrower facts.

## Principles

- Skills, CLI, MCP, and native plugins expose the same playbooks.
- The host supplies the model, context, tools, and permissions.
- Sends, publication, spend, external writes, feedback disclosure, and reusable memory retain
  the applicable human decisions.
- Credentials and private customer or workspace material stay outside public source.

## Out of scope

- Background jobs, autonomous campaigns, or provider writes started by installation.

## Signals we are on track

- Real-task usefulness, correction quality, and observed playbook use stay
  distinct from installation and fixture checks.
